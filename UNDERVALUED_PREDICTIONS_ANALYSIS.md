## Executive Summary

This analysis identifies potentially undervalued predictions in Robinhood's prediction markets (powered by Kalshi). Since Robinhood routes its prediction market trades through Kalshi's CFTC-regulated exchange, all opportunities identified here are accessible through either platform.

**Key Finding:** Several markets show pricing inefficiencies based on spread analysis, volume patterns, and probability extremes that may represent value opportunities.

---

## Methodology

Markets were evaluated on four criteria (0-25 points each, max 100):

1. **Extreme Probability Bias** - Markets at extreme prices (>90% or <10%) often overprice certainty
2. **Spread Opportunity** - Wide bid-ask spreads indicate pricing uncertainty
3. **Liquidity Score** - Low volume markets may have stale/inefficient pricing
4. **Time Pressure** - Markets near expiry may have mispriced time value

## Arbitrage & Cross-Platform Opportunities

### Platform Fee Comparison

| Platform | Fee Structure |
|----------|--------------|
| Polymarket US | 0.01% on trades |
| Kalshi/Robinhood | ~0.7% (up to 2%, capped at $1.74 per $100) |
| Polymarket International | 2% on net winnings |

### Finding Arbitrage

When the same market exists on multiple platforms:
1. Calculate total cost to buy all outcomes
2. If total < $1.00 (minus fees), arbitrage exists
3. Factor in platform-specific fees

**Example methodology:**
- Buy YES on Platform A at 45 cents
- Buy NO on Platform B at 52 cents
- Total: 97 cents = 3% gross profit
- After ~1.5% average fees = ~1.5% net profit (if prices hold)
