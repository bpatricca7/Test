## Key Lessons from Price Movement Analysis

### 1. **Fast Movers Correct Quickly**
- Bears moved +65% in ~2 weeks after strong performance
- By the time analysis is published, obvious mispricings often close

### 2. **Declining Odds Despite Better Fundamentals = Red Flag**
- S&P odds dropped 27% → 18% even as index got closer to target
- Informed money may see risks outsiders miss

### 3. **Low-Volume Markets Stay Stale**
- AFC contenders at 1-8¢ haven't moved
- Could be value OR could be correctly priced illiquid contracts

### 4. **Time Decay Matters**
- Bitcoin markets show rapid repricing as expiration approaches
- 3 days left makes all Dec 31 markets highly time-sensitive

### 5. **Head-to-Head Results Move Markets Fast**
- Seahawks beating Rams caused immediate 3-4¢ swing in both directions
- Single game results can create/destroy value quickly
