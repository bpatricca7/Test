## Caption

Your phone is now a remote control for your computer. 🤯

Codex Remote lives inside the ChatGPT app. Pair it once with a QR code and you can start tasks, watch progress, see screenshots and approve steps from anywhere — while the work runs on your real machine, with your files, apps and logins.

Add computer use and Codex can see your screen, click and type in desktop apps. Yes, that includes opening Claude and handing it a job.

How to set it up:
1️⃣ Open ChatGPT on your Mac or PC → Settings → Connections
2️⃣ Turn on "Control this Mac / PC" and scan the QR code with ChatGPT on your phone
3️⃣ Open the Remote tab on your phone and tell it what to do

Good to know: your computer has to stay awake and online, computer use can't drive terminal apps, and voice needs a paid plan.

Save this for when you set it up, and follow for more AI you can actually use.
