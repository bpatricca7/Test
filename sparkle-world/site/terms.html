How we handle your family's information is in the Privacy Notice, which is part of these terms.

The Glimmer World Membership

The plan is for one family: up to 6 children, their worlds saved on every device they play on, playing with friends, and the walkie-talkie (the last two turned on by you, for each child).

There is nothing to buy inside the game, ever. No in-app purchases, no extra items for money, no ads. Sparkle Coins are earned by playing only. Children never see a price in the game; everything about money is on the Family page, for grown-ups.

Free trial

There is no free trial. The first monthly payment is taken when you start the plan, and that first payment is also how we confirm that a grown-up agreed to playing with friends and the walkie-talkie. If we ever offer a free trial, these terms will say how long it lasts and when the first payment happens, before anyone can start one.

Renewal every month

Your plan renews automatically every month at the price above until you cancel. Each month the card you gave is charged on the same day of the month as your first payment, and Stripe (our payment company) emails you a receipt. Once a year we also email you a reminder of the plan, its price and how to cancel.

Cancelling

You can cancel any time, more easily than you started: on the Family page, tap Cancel the plan and then Yes, cancel it. No code from your email is needed (starting a plan needs one). You can also cancel in Stripe's Customer Portal (Manage subscription), or email us and we will cancel it for you.

Cancelling takes effect at the end of the current billing period. Glimmer World keeps working until then and you are not charged again. There are no partial refunds: we don't refund part of a month, unless the law where you live requires it. Until the period ends you can change your mind with Resume.

If you delete your family's account on the Family page, the plan is cancelled at once, with no further charges.

Payments that don't go through

If a monthly payment fails, Stripe tells you by email and tries again over the next days. Playing continues for 7 days after the first failed payment, so there is time to update your card on the Family page. If every try fails, the plan ends.

When a plan ends

When a plan ends, Glimmer World "rests": your children can still play and see their worlds, but what they change is kept only on their device, not on our server. We keep their worlds for 90 days so you can restart the plan or download them on the Family page, and we email you before they are deleted. The details are in the Privacy Notice.

United States only

For now the Glimmer World Membership is offered only to families in the United States, in US dollars, and the billing address must be in the United States. If a plan is started with a billing address elsewhere, we cancel it at once and refund any payment.

Kind play

Glimmer World is a gentle place for children. Please help us keep it that way:

  • Choose a nickname for each child that is not her real name and is kind to others.
  • Decide for each child whether she may play with friends and use the walkie-talkie. You are responsible for how your children use the game.
  • Don't try to get into other families' accounts or games, break or overload the service, copy it with automated tools, or get around its safety features.

If an account is used to harm others or the service, we may turn off features for it or close it. If we close an account without a good reason on your side, we refund the unused part of the month.

The game itself, its pictures and its music belong to us or to the people who licensed them to us (see the third-party notices). While your plan is active you may use them to play Glimmer World.
