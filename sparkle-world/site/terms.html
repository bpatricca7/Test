The Family Plan

There is one plan: the Sparkle World Family Plan, {{SW_PRICE_TEXT}}. It covers up to 6 children in one family: their worlds saved on every device, playing together with friends whose families have the plan too, and the walkie-talkie if you switch it on.

Free trial: a new family can start with {{SW_TRIAL_DAYS}} days free. Your card is needed at the start; the first payment is made when the trial ends, unless you cancel before then. Start now on the Family page ends the trial early and pays at once.

There is no free trial: the first payment is made when you start the plan.

For now the Family Plan is only for families who live in the United States. Payments are handled by Stripe.

Renewal and cancelling

  • It renews automatically every month at the same price, plus sales tax where it applies, until you cancel. Stripe emails you a receipt each time.
  • Cancel any time, online: Family page, Manage subscription, then cancel. It takes effect at the end of the month you already paid for, and nothing more is charged. There are no partial refunds for the rest of a month.
  • Once a year we email you a reminder of the plan, its price and how to cancel.
  • If a payment fails, the game keeps working for a few days while Stripe tries again, and you can update your card on the Family page.
  • After a plan ends, your children's worlds are kept for a while so you can come back (see the Privacy Notice), or you can delete everything at once.

Grown-ups and children

The account belongs to a parent or legal guardian who is 18 or older, and who agrees to these terms and to the Privacy Notice for the children who play. Children never need an email and never see a price. You are responsible for who can use the devices you set up for your family.

Nothing to buy inside the game

There are no in-app purchases, ever. Sparkle Coins are earned by playing (finding gems, cooking, gardening, a daily gift) and can't be bought or exchanged for money. The Family Plan is the only thing that costs money, and only a grown-up can start it, on the Family page.

Playing kindly

  • Play nicely with friends: the host's world is hers, and so are her rules.
  • Use a nickname, not a real name, and kind names for worlds and pets.
  • Don't try to break, overload or get around the game or its safety checks, or to get into anyone else's account.

We may pause or end an account that puts other children at risk, and we will tell the grown-up why.

Changes

If we change these terms or the price, we tell you by email at least 30 days before, and a new price applies only from your next month after that. You can cancel before it does. The game is provided as it is; we work hard to keep it running and your children's worlds safe, and we keep copies of them on our server and on your devices.
