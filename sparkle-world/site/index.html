A cozy building game for kids

Build a whole world. Then move in.

Glimmer World is a soft, pastel world made of blocks. Build a house, fill it with really nice beds and everything else, dress up, adopt a unicorn, and invite a friend over with a secret code made of pictures.

What's inside

Everything on the wish list.

Glimmer World started with a list from a girl who is almost 8: make my own worlds, cool outfits, really nice houses with beds in them, pets, candy and ice cream, zip lines, treehouses, and big campers you can go inside. So that is what's inside. Then she asked for boys to play with, and for cars, vans and boats to drive, so they are inside too.

Build your own worlds

Pick a world. Build anything!

Start a world in one of seven kinds of places, then build with more than 200 pastel blocks: pink planks, heart windows, glowing lamps, candy, glitter and rainbow blocks. Hold still for a moment, then drag, to build a whole row at once. Oops? There is always Undo.

03

Make it home

My bed has a canopy!

Really nice beds (a princess canopy bed, bunk beds, a heart bed, a cloud bed) and every kind of furniture, in lots of colors. Play the piano. Watch cartoons on the TV. Fill the bath with bubbles. Read a storybook. When night comes, the lamps really light up the room, and a bed means sweet dreams until morning.

04

Dress up

A tiara, fairy wings and space buns!

The Dress-Up Studio has 19 hair styles, from braids and space buns to buzz cuts and faux hawks, tops, jerseys, skirts, dresses, shorts and shoes, hats, caps and tiaras, glasses, wings and backpacks, and something to hold. Everything comes in lots of colors and patterns: hearts, stars, stripes, dots, plaid, dinos, rockets, rainbow and flowers. Or tap Surprise me! and pick Girl, Boy or Mix.

05

Pets to love

I can ride my unicorn in the sky!

Adopt puppies and kitties in lots of breeds, bunnies, a panda, ducklings and turtles that love to swim. Give them names, pet them, feed them treats. The pony and the horse are for riding, and the unicorn flies, leaving a rainbow trail behind it.

06

Invite friends from a group of ten girls and six boys, each with their own style. They chat with you, dance with you and sleep over.

    Questions

    Good questions.

    Who is it for?

    It was made for a girl who is almost 8, so every button has a picture and a short word, and nothing is scary or can be lost. For early readers, the game can read its words out loud (switch on Read words out loud in Settings), and the storybooks have a Read to me button. Kids who love building, dressing up, pets and adventures will feel right at home.

    Do we need to download anything?

    No. Glimmer World runs in the web browser on iPad, phones and computers. Tap Play now and it starts. It works with a mouse and keyboard, or with touch.

    Can strangers talk to my child?

    There is no typing: players tap one of 16 friendly phrases. To visit a world, a friend needs the 4-picture code, and the host has to tap Let in! for each friend. The only voice is the walkie-talkie, which stays off until a grown-up turns it on for that device; then only friends who were let in, and have theirs on too, can hear. The host can mute anyone, or send anyone home, at any time.

    How does the walkie-talkie work?

    A grown-up opens Settings, taps Walkie-talkie (grown-ups) and answers a multiplication question. That turns it on for this device only; each family decides for their own. While playing together, your child holds the walkie button to talk (up to 15 seconds), and the microphone turns off as soon as they let go. Friends in the same game who have theirs on hear them live. Nothing is recorded or kept anywhere, and it works only on this website, not in other versions of the game.

    Is there anything to buy? Any ads?

    No and no. The candy shop and the ice cream parlor use Sparkle Coins, which come from playing: finding gems, cooking, gardening, a daily gift. Coins can't be bought.

    Where are the worlds saved?

    On your device, in the browser, saved as you play. Nothing is stored on the server. Browsers can clear website data, and Safari may clear it for sites that go unused for a while, so save a favorite world as a file now and then: open My Worlds and tap Save on the world. That file also moves a world to another device (My Worlds, then Open a file).

    Can strangers talk to my child?

    There is no typing: players tap one of 16 friendly phrases. To visit a world, a friend needs the 4-picture code, and the host has to tap Let in! for each friend. The only voice is the walkie-talkie, which stays off until a grown-up switches it on; then only friends who were let in, and have theirs on too, can hear. The host can mute anyone, or send anyone home, at any time.

    How does the walkie-talkie work?

    A grown-up switches it on for each child on the Family page (it needs Play with friends on too, and your first payment is how we confirm a grown-up said yes). While playing together, your child holds the walkie button to talk (up to 15 seconds), and the microphone turns off as soon as they let go. Friends in the same game who have theirs on hear them live. Nothing is recorded or kept anywhere, and it works only on this website, not in other versions of the game.

    On a device that isn't signed in yet, it works the old way: a grown-up opens Settings, taps Walkie-talkie (grown-ups) and answers a multiplication question, for that device only.

    Is there anything to buy? Any ads?

    No ads, and nothing to buy inside the game. The Family Plan ({{SW_PRICE_TEXT}}) is the only thing that costs money, and only a grown-up can start it, on the Family page. The candy shop and the ice cream parlor use Sparkle Coins, which come from playing: finding gems, cooking, gardening, a daily gift. Coins can't be bought, and kids never see a price.

    Where are the worlds saved?

    On the device, in the browser, as your child plays, and with the Family Plan also in your family's cloud copy on our server, so their worlds follow them to another device and come back if a browser clears them. You can download them, or delete them, any time on the Family page. My Worlds, then Save, still saves a world as a file too.

    What does a child need to sign in?

    Nothing: kids never type an email or a password. A grown-up signs in on the Family page with an email code, adds each child as a nickname, and sets up the child's device with a short code (or taps Kids play on this device). On the device, the child taps their picture in Who's playing? and plays.

    How do I cancel the Family Plan?

    On the Family page: Cancel the plan, then Yes, cancel it. No code, no questions. It keeps working until the end of the month you paid for, and nothing more is charged. You can download everything, or delete your family's account, on the Family page too.

    How many friends can play together?

    Up to 4 players in one world, each on their own device. The friend who made the code is the host: it's their world, and their rules.

    What if a friend knocks down my house?

    With Careful players on (it starts on), friends can only change their own building and the ground, not your houses and furniture. The host can also Undo building for a friend, and a copy of your world from before friends came is kept on your device.
