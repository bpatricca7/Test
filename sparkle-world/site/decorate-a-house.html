Glimmer World guides

Build and decorate your first house.

Start with a furnished Magic House, or build your own from pastel blocks. Then make one room feel like yours with a favorite color, a bed and a few small details.

Choose a world and a house

Before you start, a grown-up sets up the family membership and the child's device. Membership costs {{SW_PRICE_TEXT}}, renews monthly until canceled, and includes everything in the game. There is no free trial.

Tap Play now and choose a world. Builder Flat is a good first place to try because its ground is flat. Flower Meadow gives you hills, flowers and cherry blossom trees.

For a quick start, open your Bag and choose a Magic House. Choose a clear spot and tap Build! to place a furnished home. The Flower Cottage is a simple starting idea; the Sparkle Camper has bunk beds inside and a rooftop deck.

Make a house from blocks

To build your own, pick blocks from the Bag, select Build, and place a floor, walls and windows. Start with one small room. Leave an opening where you want the entrance before adding the roof.

Pink planks, heart windows and heart wallpaper make a cozy cottage. Rainbow blocks and glowing lamps give it a different look. Hold still for a moment, then drag to build a whole row. Use Undo when you want to change the last step.

Decorate one room at a time

Pick two main colors, then choose furniture from your Bag. Start with the biggest item, such as a bed or sofa. Leave some clear floor around it so the room has space to move through.

Add one useful detail and one playful detail: a lamp beside a bed, a bookshelf near a sofa, or a cake in the kitchen. For furniture that offers colors, use Pick a color! to choose the version you like.

If you started with a Magic House, its beds and furniture are already there. Change one corner first instead of rebuilding every room at once.

Three ideas to try next

  • A reading corner: a sofa, bookshelf and lamp in your favorite colors.
  • A camper bedroom: begin with the Sparkle Camper, explore its bunks and kitchen, and personalize the inside.
  • A friendship garden: make two flower patches joined by a path, with a spot to sit in the middle.

For the first build theme, try a friendship garden. Each player creates their own patch; choose colors together and finish with a tour. There is no deadline or prize to claim.

Invite a friend for a house tour

A grown-up can help arrange a visit with a family you know. Each friend's family needs a membership when family accounts are required. Use the four-picture code and approve each arrival with Let in!.

Keep Careful players on to protect the host's house and furniture. You can switch Players can build off for a tour. Follow the parent playdate guide for setup and controls.

If a grown-up shares a picture of the build, keep it to the game world. Avoid including children's real names, faces or the live invite code.
