Privacy Notice

What we keep, and why.

Glimmer World is a game for children, so the law that protects children's information online in the United States (COPPA, the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) applies to all of it. This page says, in plain words, what we collect, why, who helps us, how long we keep it, and what you can do about it.

Any question about this notice or your children's information goes to that address. We are the only operator that collects or keeps children's information through Glimmer World.

The short version

  • Accounts belong to grown-ups. A parent signs in with an email address. Children never type an email, a real name or anything else about themselves.
  • For each child we keep a nickname you choose, her avatar and its picture, her game progress and her worlds, only to run the game for her.
  • Playing with friends and the walkie-talkie are off until you switch them on for each child. Voices are never recorded.
  • No ads, no analytics, no trackers. We never sell or share information for advertising.
  • You can see, download and delete everything on the Family page, any time.

What we keep about children, and why

With your permission, for each child you add to your family:

  • A nickname that you choose on the Family page (up to 12 letters; please a nickname, not her real name). It shows her player in the game. The game runs it through a friendly-words filter before anyone else sees it.
  • Her avatar (how she dressed up her player) and a small picture of her avatar's head (a portrait, drawn by the game, never a photo). They show her player in the game, in the "Who's playing?" picker and on the Family page.
  • Her game progress: stickers, Sparkle Coins (earned in the game only; nothing is ever sold), outfits, stats such as how many things she has built or cooked, and her game settings.
  • Her worlds, including the names she types for her worlds and her pets. We keep them on our server so they follow her from one device to another and come back if a browser clears the game's storage.

How we use it: only to run the game for her: to save and load her worlds, show her player, and let her play with friends when you allow it. Never for advertising, profiling or anything else.

If a child plays without an account (for example on a device nobody has signed in on, or inside the claude.ai app), her worlds stay on that device only and nothing about her reaches our server, apart from the live game connection while she plays with friends.

What we never collect

We never collect children's email addresses, real names, birthdays, photos, location, contacts or recordings. The camera is never used. There is no chat typing in the game (children can tap one of 16 friendly phrases), so there are no messages to keep. We don't use analytics, ads, error-tracking tools or anything that follows a child around the internet, and we don't track how long anyone plays.

What we keep about grown-ups

  • Your email address, so you can sign in and so we can ask your permission and send the emails listed on the Family page (sign-in codes, a confirmation of your choices, plan reminders). We never send marketing emails, and our emails never contain your children's nicknames, avatars, worlds or pet names.
  • Records of your choices: when you agreed to this notice and which version, when a payment confirmed that a grown-up said yes, and each time a child's Play with friends or walkie-talkie switch was turned on or off. These records hold dates and versions only.
  • Your plan: whether it is active, its renewal date, and the country of your billing address (we sell in the United States only). Your card number and your full billing address are kept by Stripe, not by us.
  • Your devices: a rough name for each signed-in device (like "iPad · Safari", or a name you give it; please use a nickname there too, never a child's real name) and when it was last used, so you can sign any of them out.

Playing with friends and the walkie-talkie

Children can make some information visible to other children only through these two switches, which you control for each child on the Family page. Both are off until you turn them on, and you can turn them off again at any time; a game in progress follows the switch within seconds.

  • Play with friends. When it is on, the other players in a game she joins or hosts see her nickname, her avatar and the world. Only friends the host lets in with a tap can come into a game, and only children whose families also have Glimmer World. There is no typing: only 16 friendly phrases.
  • Walkie-talkie (only together with Play with friends). When it is on, the friends in the same game hear her voice live while she holds the walkie button, for at most 15 seconds at a time. Voices go through our server to the other players and are forgotten at once: they are never recorded or stored anywhere.

You can agree to us keeping her worlds without agreeing to playing with friends. The two switches become available once we have confirmed that a grown-up said yes: your first payment for the Glimmer World Membership does that (see How we ask for your permission).

Cookies and other identifiers

Glimmer World uses a few persistent identifiers, only to support the internal operations of the game (signing in, keeping a device signed in, saving, knowing who is who in a game, security and fair limits), never for advertising or profiling:

  • One sign-in cookie (__Host-sw_sess) keeps a signed-in device signed in: a parent's browser, or a child's device a parent set up. While a grown-up is signing in, a second short-lived cookie (__Host-sw_login, 15 minutes) makes sure the code works only in the browser that asked for it.
  • A device secret kept in the game's own storage on each device. When playing together, our server turns it into a stamp that lets the host's game recognise a friend's device in that one game (so a friend doesn't have to knock again after a reload). The stamp is different in every game, so nobody can follow a device from game to game.
  • Internet (IP) addresses are used only in the server's memory, for a few minutes, to limit how many connections one home can open. They are never stored or logged.

There are no advertising or analytics cookies, and the pages and the game load nothing from other companies' websites.

Who helps us run it

These service providers work for us under their data processing terms. They may use the information only to provide their service to us.

  • Railway (railway.com) hosts the game and its database, so it stores everything described on this page on our behalf.
  • Stripe (stripe.com) takes the payments for the Glimmer World Membership and calculates sales tax. It receives your email address, your card details and your billing address, which you type on Stripe's own page. It never receives your children's information.
  • Our email provider (Resend, Postmark or Microsoft 365, whichever we use) sends our emails. It receives your email address and the text of our emails, which never contain children's information.

We never sell or rent information, and we don't share it for advertising. Glimmer World has no ads. We would share information with someone else only if the law requires it (for example a court order) or to protect a child's safety, and we would tell you when the law allows it.

How long we keep things

We keep children's information only as long as it is needed to run the game for them, then we delete it. Our written retention policy:

  • While your plan is active, we keep your children's players, progress and worlds.
  • After your plan ends, we keep them for 90 days so you can come back or download them. We email you 30 days and 7 days before they are deleted. Then they are deleted.
  • Your email address and your plan details are kept for 12 months after your plan ends (so you can come back to the same account), then deleted together with your Stripe customer record.
  • If you don't finish setting up (you never agree to this notice), we delete your email address after 14 days. If you agreed but never started a plan and added no children, after 30 days.
  • The records of your choices (dates, versions, switches; no names or emails) are kept for 3 years after they are made, as proof of your consent.
  • Deleted worlds leave a small marker for 30 days so other devices remove their copy too.
  • Sign-in codes and links are deleted a day after they expire. A parent's browser stays signed in for at most 30 days (14 days unused); a child's device for 180 days after it was last used.
  • Walkie-talkie voices are never stored.
  • Backups of our database are kept for at most 7 days, then roll off.
  • When you delete a world, a child or your whole family, it is deleted from our server at once, and from the backups within 7 days.

Stripe keeps its own payment records for as long as the law requires it to (for example for taxes); they contain no information about your children.

What you can do

As a parent or legal guardian you can, at any time and free of charge:

  • Review what we keep about each child: See her data on the Family page shows her worlds (with pictures, sizes and dates), stickers, coins and stats.
  • Download her worlds (they open in the game with Open a file) or everything about your family in one file.
  • Delete one world (in the game), one child (Delete player), or your whole family (Delete our account). Deleting a child or the family also stops any further collection.
  • Turn a switch off (Play with friends, walkie-talkie): this revokes that permission at once, including in a game in progress.
  • Ask us by email at {{SW_OPERATOR_EMAIL}} to do any of this. We first confirm the request from the email address of your account, then answer within 10 business days. We never send children's information by email: for a copy, we help you sign in and use Download everything on the Family page.

To protect your children, downloads, deletions and switching things on ask for a fresh code from your email first.

How we ask for your permission

We need your permission first: if you don't agree, we don't collect, use or share anything about your children. Before any information about a child is kept, a grown-up signs in with a code sent to her email, reads a short notice (the same points as this page) and ticks "I agree". The next day we send an email confirming that choice, with a way to change your mind.

For playing with friends and the walkie-talkie, which let other children see and hear your child, we need a stronger confirmation that a grown-up said yes. We use the card payment method: your first payment for the Glimmer World Membership ({{SW_PRICE_TEXT}}), made with a card whose owner is told about every charge. Until then both switches stay off. Families with a free pass can instead send a signed consent form, or confirm on a phone or video call.

How we keep it safe

We follow a written information security program. In short: every connection is encrypted; the database is reachable only inside our host's private network; sign-in codes, links and cookies are random, single-use where possible, and stored only in scrambled (hashed) form; every account that runs the service is protected with two-step sign-in; our server logs never contain email addresses, nicknames, world names, codes or internet addresses; and every change to the server is tested before it goes live. If something ever went wrong, we would tell affected families and follow the law on breach notices.

Changes to this notice

If we change what we collect or how we use it, we update this page first, and before a change that matters takes effect we ask you to agree again the next time you sign in. We never use information in a new way without your permission.
