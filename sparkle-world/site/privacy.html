Sparkle World is directed to children under 13, so the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) applies. We ask a parent before we keep any child's information, keep as little as we can, and use it only to run the game.

What we keep about children, and why

Only with a parent's permission, given on the Family page, and only to run the game for her:

  • A nickname that the parent chooses (up to 12 letters, through a word filter): to show her player. Please use a nickname, not a real name.
  • Her avatar's look and a small picture of its head: to show her player in the game, on the family's other devices and on the Family page.
  • Her game progress (stickers, Sparkle Coins, outfits, counters and settings): so her game is the same on every device.
  • Her worlds, including names she types for worlds and pets: saved on our server so they follow her between devices and survive a browser clearing its data. Names other children could see go through the word filter.

We use this information only to provide the game (internal operations). We never use it for advertising, profiles or anything else.

What we never collect

Children's emails, real names, birthdays, photos, location, contacts, voice recordings or typed messages (there is no chat typing). No ads, no analytics, no trackers, no error-tracking services. The camera is never used. Internet addresses are used only in the server's memory, to limit how often one address can connect, and are never stored.

What other children can see

Two switches on the Family page decide, for each child, and both start off:

  • Play with friends. When on, the other players in a game she joins or hosts (only friends the host lets in, whose families also have Sparkle World) see her nickname, her avatar and the world. There is no typing, only 16 friendly phrases.
  • Walkie-talkie. When on (it also needs Play with friends), those friends hear her voice live while she holds the walkie button. Voices are relayed live and never recorded or stored.

You can agree to saving her worlds without agreeing to either switch. The switches become available after your first payment: that payment is how we confirm that a grown-up said yes. Turning a switch off reaches a game in progress within a second.

What we keep about you

  • Your email address: to ask your permission, to sign you in with a code, and to send you notices about your plan.
  • Your choices: when you agreed to our notice (and which version), when your consent was confirmed, and when you switched something on or off for a child. This record never includes a child's nickname or your email.
  • Your plan: its state and dates, and your billing country, from Stripe. Your card details go only to Stripe; we never see them.

Who helps us run it

  • Railway hosts the game and its database.
  • Stripe handles payments. It receives your email and payment details, never your children's information.
  • Our email provider sends our emails. It receives your email address and our text. Our emails never contain a child's nickname, avatar, worlds or pets.

They act for us under their data processing terms. We don't sell or share anyone's information, and we don't show ads.

Cookies and identifiers

One cookie keeps a signed-in device signed in (and a short-lived one remembers a sign-in in progress). Each device also keeps a random number so that a friend whose page reloads can come back to the same game; the server turns it into a different stamp in every game. These are used only to run the game and keep it safe, never for advertising or tracking.

How long we keep it

  • Children's information: while your plan is active, and {{SW_RETAIN_DAYS}} days after it ends (so you can come back). We email you 30 and 7 days before we delete it.
  • Your email and plan record: 12 months after a plan ends, then deleted.
  • If you sign up but don't agree to the notice within 14 days, we delete your email address.
  • Sign-in codes: until they expire. Signed-in devices: 30 days for a grown-up's browser, 180 days after last use for a kid's device.
  • The record of your choices (no personal details): 3 years, as proof of consent.
  • When you delete a child or your account, it is gone from our server at once; our backups roll off within 7 days. Stripe keeps the payment records the law requires.

Your rights as a parent

On the Family page, at any time: see what we keep for each child (See her data), download her worlds or everything, turn off any switch, delete a child (which stops any further collection), or delete your whole account. Sensitive actions ask for a fresh code from your email first.

You can also write to {{SW_OPERATOR_EMAIL}} or call {{SW_OPERATOR_PHONE}}. We confirm the request from your account's email address and answer within 10 business days.

Changes to this notice

If we change what we keep or how we use it, we update this notice and ask you again on the Family page before the change applies to your children.
