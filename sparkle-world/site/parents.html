Safety and privacy, in plain words.

Sparkle World was made by a dad for his daughter. This page says exactly what the game does, what it keeps, and where. If something here ever stops being true, this page changes with it.

The short version

  • No chat typing. Players can only tap one of 16 fixed friendly phrases.
  • No voice, no camera. The game never uses the microphone or camera.
  • No accounts. No sign-up, no emails, no passwords.
  • No ads and nothing to buy. Sparkle Coins are earned by playing and can't be bought.
  • Worlds stay on your device. The server doesn't store worlds, names, pictures or messages.
  • Nothing scary. No monsters, no fighting, no damage, nothing to lose.

Playing with friends

One player taps Play with Friends and then Make a Code (or Menu and then Invite Friends inside a world). Four big pictures appear, for example heart, star, moon, cat. There are 12 pictures, so there are 20,736 possible codes.

A friend taps Play with Friends, then Join a Code on her own device, taps the same 4 pictures, and taps Go!.

Knowing the code is not enough. The player who made the code (the host) sees a card, "Mia wants to play!", and must tap Let in! for each friend, one at a time. Not now says no.

  • Up to 4 players can be in one world.
  • The host can Send home any friend at any time. That friend can't knock again in that game.
  • Careful friends is on from the start: friends can build and change only their own things and the natural ground, not the host's houses and furniture.
  • Friends can build can be switched off, so friends can only look around and play.
  • The host can Undo building for one friend, and a copy of her world from before friends came is kept on her device (Before friends).
  • Stop playing ends the game for everyone. Her world is saved on her device.

The friends in the Bag (the girls you can invite to live in your world) are part of the game, not real people. Real people are always called Players.

Talking

There is no typing to other players at all, and no voice. Players can tap one of these 16 phrases, and do emotes like waving and dancing:

  • Hi!
  • Come here!
  • Look!
  • Follow me!
  • Let's build!
  • Wow!
  • So pretty!
  • Thank you!
  • Help please!
  • Let's dance!
  • Race you!
  • Yes!
  • No thanks
  • Oops!
  • Good night!
  • Bye!

Only a number for the phrase travels to the other devices; each device shows the words from its own list. The only things a player types are names (her own, her pets' and her worlds'), and names go through a word filter before other players see them.

What is saved, and where

On your device: worlds, outfits, stickers and settings are saved in the web browser on the device she plays on, as she plays. They are not sent anywhere. Clearing the browser's website data for this site removes them.

Moving a world: open My Worlds, tap Save on a world (it saves a file), then on the other device open My Worlds, Open a file, and pick that file.

On the server: nothing is stored. When friends play together, their game exists only in the server's memory while they play, and disappears when they leave (or after 10 quiet minutes). The server does not save worlds, names, pictures or messages, and does not log what happens in games. Each device makes up a random number for itself, so a friend whose page reloads can come back to the same game.

What the pages load

This site has no ads, no analytics and no trackers, and it doesn't set cookies. The home page and the game load one thing from outside: the rounded Fredoka font, from Google Fonts. That means your browser asks Google's font servers for the font file. If that request is blocked, the pages still work with a built-in font.

The game is built with the open-source three.js library; its license is in the third-party notices.

The web address

Like any website, anyone who has the address can open the game, and could try codes. They would still need a Let in! from the host to come into her world. Share the address with the families you play with.

Limits on the server: at most 4 players per game, and a small number of connections from one home internet connection. Other websites can't connect to the game server.

Devices

Sparkle World runs in the web browser on iPad, phones and computers. There is nothing to download or install. It works with touch, or with a mouse and keyboard.

To keep it like an app on an iPad or iPhone: open the game with Play now, tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.
