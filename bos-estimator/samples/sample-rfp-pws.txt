BASE OPERATIONS SUPPORT (BOS) SERVICES — FORT EXAMPLE, KENTUCKY
PERFORMANCE WORK STATEMENT (PWS) — EXCERPT WITH TECHNICAL EXHIBITS

SECTION 1 — GENERAL
1.1 Scope. The Contractor shall provide all personnel, equipment, tools, materials, supervision, and other items and services necessary to perform Custodial Services, Floor Care, Grounds Maintenance, and Full Food Service (FFS) at the Dining Facility at Fort Example, Kentucky, as defined in this PWS.
1.2 Period of Performance. The contract consists of a base period of twelve (12) months and four (4) one-year option periods.
1.3 Climate. Fort Example is in a temperate climate with a growing season of approximately 30 weeks (April through October). Snow and ice removal is required for walks and parking areas on an as-needed basis (historical average 6 events per year).
1.4 Hours. Administrative facilities are cleaned after normal duty hours (1700–0130). Barracks common areas, the Fitness Center, the Health Clinic, and the Dining Facility require seven-day-per-week service.
1.5 Current Contract. These services are currently performed under contract W912QR-20-C-0015, awarded in September 2020 to Bluegrass Facility Services, LLC, a firm-fixed-price contract with a total value of $31,850,000 over a 60-month period of performance. Obligations through the end of the fourth performance year total $26,100,000. The incumbent contract expires 30 September 2026.

SECTION 5 — CUSTODIAL SERVICES
5.1 The Contractor shall provide custodial services in the facilities listed in Technical Exhibit 3 (TE-3). Square footage shown is gross square footage (GSF) unless a cleanable square footage (CSF) is listed. Service frequency is shown per facility.
5.2 Routine services include emptying waste receptacles, vacuuming carpet, dust and damp mopping hard floors, cleaning and disinfecting restrooms and restocking supplies, spot cleaning glass, doors and partitions, and policing entrances.
5.3 Periodic floor care includes spray buffing and scrub/recoat of resilient flooring, annual strip and refinish, carpet interim cleaning quarterly and hot-water extraction semi-annually, and machine scrubbing of ceramic tile.

SECTION 6 — GROUNDS MAINTENANCE
6.1 The Contractor shall maintain the grounds listed in Technical Exhibit 4 (TE-4). Improved grounds shall be mowed weekly during the growing season to a height of 3 inches, trimmed and edged with each mowing. Semi-improved grounds shall be mowed every three weeks. Unimproved grounds shall be rotary-cut three times per year.
6.2 Planting beds shall be weeded, cultivated and kept free of debris; beds shall be re-mulched annually. Shrubs shall be pruned twice per year. Trees under 30 feet shall be pruned on a three-year cycle.
6.3 Parking lots shall be mechanically swept monthly; sidewalks shall be blown and kept free of litter.

SECTION 7 — FULL FOOD SERVICE, DINING FACILITY
7.1 The Contractor shall operate Dining Facility Bldg 2410 (Warrior Restaurant) providing full food service: receiving and storage, food preparation, serving line and short-order operation, dining room attendant services, warewashing, and sanitation of all food service areas.
7.2 Workload. The facility serves an average of 2,650 meals per day across four meal periods (breakfast, lunch, dinner, and midnight meal), seven days per week, 365 days per year. Seating capacity is 450 seats. The kitchen and serving area is approximately 11,000 SF and the dining room is approximately 12,500 SF.
7.3 Sanitation. The dining room floors and tables shall be cleaned after each meal period. Kitchen floors, hoods, and equipment shall be sanitized daily in accordance with TB MED 530.
