PERFORMANCE WORK STATEMENT
Custodial, Grounds Maintenance and Snow and Ice Removal Services – U.S. Army Reserve Centers, Northern Plains Cluster

Part 1 – General Information

1.1 GENERAL. This is a non-personal services contract to provide custodial, grounds maintenance and snow and ice removal services in support of the 89th Readiness Division (RD). The Government shall not exercise any supervision or control over the contract service providers performing the services herein. Such contract service providers shall be accountable solely to the Contractor who, in turn, is responsible to the Government.

1.2 DESCRIPTION OF SERVICES. The Contractor shall provide all personnel, supervision, equipment, tools, materials, cleaning chemicals, floor finish, fuel, transportation and other items necessary to perform custodial services in the buildings listed in Technical Exhibit 2 (TE-2), and grounds maintenance and snow and ice removal on the sites listed in Technical Exhibit 3 (TE-3), except for those items specified as Government-furnished property and services (Part 3). The Government will furnish toilet tissue, paper towels, hand soap and can liners.

1.3 BACKGROUND. Army Reserve Centers (USARCs) are used by Troop Program Unit (TPU) Soldiers during monthly Battle Assembly (BA) weekends and by full-time Active Guard Reserve (AGR) and civilian staff during the work week. A typical site has a training center, an Organizational Maintenance Shop (OMS) or Area Maintenance Support Activity (AMSA), and an unheated storage building. Two sites are Armed Forces Reserve Centers (AFRCs) shared with the Army National Guard; National Guard tenant areas are cleaned by the State and are excluded from the Custodial SF in TE-2.

1.4 CURRENT CONTRACT. These services are currently performed by Red River Prairie Facility Services, LLC under Contract No. W91XPN-22-C-0009, awarded on 18 March 2022 for a twelve-month base period (1 April 2022 – 31 March 2023) and four twelve-month option periods ending 31 March 2027. The total value of the contract including all option periods is $5,912,400 for sixty (60) months. Option Period 4 (1 April 2026 – 31 March 2027) was exercised at $1,214,880. The current contract covers the same twelve base sites; the Watertown USARC (SD033) is not under contract.

1.5 PERIOD OF PERFORMANCE. The period of performance shall be a twelve-month base period from 1 April 2027 through 31 March 2028 and four (4) twelve-month option periods, exercised at the discretion of the Government (Option Period 4 ends 31 March 2032). FAR 52.217-8, Option to Extend Services (up to six months), is incorporated; any extension under that clause is not an option period.

1.6 PLACE OF PERFORMANCE. The work shall be performed at the twelve (12) base sites listed in TE-1. The optional site listed in TE-1 (Watertown USARC, SD033) shall be priced as option line items and may be added by modification during the base period; it is not part of the base requirement. TE-2 lists every building at each site; buildings shown with routine frequency “N/S” (no service) are identified for grounds, snow removal and site orientation only and receive no custodial service.

1.7 HOURS OF OPERATION. Routine custodial services shall be performed between 0700 and 1730 hours, Monday through Friday, excluding Federal holidays. Battle Assembly weekend services (TE-2 column “BA Wknd”) shall be performed on the Sunday of each BA weekend after 1300 hours, or as coordinated with the unit; the RFOS will furnish the BA schedule for each site (an estimated twelve BA weekends per site per year). Grounds work shall be performed during daylight hours Monday through Saturday. Snow and ice removal shall be performed when ordered, at any hour.

1.8 RECOGNIZED HOLIDAYS. New Year’s Day, Martin Luther King Jr.’s Birthday, Presidents Day, Memorial Day, Juneteenth National Independence Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Columbus Day, Veterans Day, Thanksgiving Day and Christmas Day. When a holiday falls on a Sunday, the following Monday is observed; when it falls on a Saturday, the preceding Friday is observed.

Part 3 – Government-Furnished Property and Services

3.1 The Government will furnish, at no cost, utilities (water and electricity) for performance of the work, one lockable janitor closet per training center for storage of the Contractor’s supplies and equipment, and the consumable paper products, hand soap and can liners listed in 1.2. Dumpsters and refuse collection are provided under a separate contract.

Part 4 – Contractor-Furnished Items and Responsibilities

4.1 The Contractor shall furnish all equipment, vehicles and materials, including mowers, string trimmers, edgers, blowers, snow plows, snow blowers, sand, salt and ice melt, in adequate numbers and of appropriate type and size. All cleaning chemicals shall be EPA Safer Choice or Green Seal certified where available. Safety Data Sheets shall be submitted to the COR within 10 days after contract award.

4.2 The Contractor shall submit a Quality Control Plan within 15 calendar days after award and a Cleaning Frequency Schedule for each site within 30 days after contract start, showing the days on which each routine and periodic service will be performed.

4.3 The Contractor is responsible for any damage to Government property caused by its operations, including turf, shrubs, curbs, fences, gates and handrails damaged by mowing, plowing or salt.

Part 5 – Specific Tasks

The routine frequency applies year-round. Buildings marked “N/S” receive no routine or periodic custodial service. Price custodial services on the Custodial SF; Gross SF is provided for information.

5.3 BATTLE ASSEMBLY WEEKEND SERVICE. Where TE-2 column “BA Wknd” shows “Y”, the Contractor shall, in addition to the routine frequency, clean and restock latrines and locker rooms, empty trash and recycling, and spot-clean the assembly hall, break rooms and entrances on the Sunday of each BA weekend. BA weekend service is a separate monthly task and does not change the routine frequency.

5.4 ROUTINE TASKS. At each routine service the Contractor shall: empty trash and recycling containers; clean, disinfect and restock latrines, showers and locker rooms; sweep and damp-mop hard floors; vacuum carpeted areas and walk-off mats; spot-clean doors, glass and partitions; clean drinking fountains; and clean break rooms and kitchenette counters. Periodic tasks (strip and refinish VCT semi-annually; extract carpet annually; clean light fixtures and high dusting annually) are included in the routine price.

5.5 GROUNDS MAINTENANCE. The growing season is 1 May through 15 October. The Contractor shall perform the following on the areas shown in TE-3 and on the site maps (Attachment 2):

  1. Mowing – improved grounds. Mow when grass reaches four (4) inches and cut to a height of 2½ to 3 inches, uniform in appearance and free of skips, gaps, rutting or scalping. Sixteen (16) mowing events are estimated per season.
  2. Trimming. Trim around buildings, fences, signs, poles, hydrants, trees, curbs and bumper blocks concurrent with every other mowing (eight (8) trimming events per season).
  3. Edging. Edge sidewalks, curbs and paved areas twice per season (spring and mid-summer), to the linear footage shown in TE-3.
  4. Semi-improved grounds. Mow three (3) times per season to a height of 4 to 6 inches.
  5. Clean-up. Remove clippings from sidewalks, curbs, parking areas and roadways immediately after each mowing, trimming and edging event. Pick up litter before each mowing.

Mowed and maintained areas are approximate and were scaled from the 89th RD site maps; the Contractor shall field-verify areas during the site visit.

5.6 SNOW AND ICE REMOVAL. The snow season is 15 October through 30 April. Snow and ice removal is performed on call. The Contractor shall arrive on site and begin work within two (2) hours of notification by the RFOS or COR. POV parking lots, roads and drives shall be plowed to bare pavement with no trailing windrows, catch basins and hydrants cleared, and entrances plowed back four (4) feet from the curb line for traffic visibility. Sidewalks, ramps, landings and entrance slabs shall be cleared at least at each four (4) inch snowfall interval and sanded or salted at the end of each storm. MEP areas are not plowed under this contract. The Contractor shall e-mail time-stamped before-and-after photographs to the RFOS after each event; invoices will not be approved without photographs. An estimated eighteen (18) plowing events and twenty-four (24) sidewalk clearing / salting events per site per season are used for pricing.

5.7 ADDITIONS AND DELETIONS. The Government may add or delete buildings, sites or areas by modification. Price adjustments for custodial services will be made on the basis of the Custodial SF added or deleted, multiplied by the offeror’s price per square foot for the applicable frequency.

Item 6 – Questions and Answers

Q1. Should custodial be priced on Gross SF or Custodial SF? A1. Custodial SF (TE-2 Note 3; PWS 5.1). Gross SF is from the real property inventory and includes mechanical rooms, vaults, maintenance bays and ARNG tenant areas.

Q2. Are the unheated storage buildings marked N/S cleaned at all? A2. No. Only the unheated storage buildings marked Q (MN027-03, ND008-03, SD004-03) are swept quarterly. N/S buildings are listed so that offerors can relate TE-2 to the site maps and TE-3.

Q3. Is the MEP plowed or swept? A3. No. MEP is shown in TE-3 for information only (Note 4). Units clear their own MEP.

Q4. Will the Watertown USARC (SD033) be awarded? A4. SD033 is an optional site (TE-1, line O-1). It is evaluated but is not part of the base requirement and will be added only by modification if the Government elects to do so.

Q5. Who is the incumbent and what is the current price? A5. See PWS 1.4: Red River Prairie Facility Services, LLC, Contract No. W91XPN-22-C-0009 (awarded 18 March 2022). The current option period (1 April 2026 – 31 March 2027) is $1,214,880; the total value for the 60-month contract is $5,912,400.

Q6. Does “BA Wknd” service replace a weekday service? A6. No. It is in addition to the routine frequency (PWS 5.3).

Q7. The Fergus Falls VMS appears in TE-1 as part of the building count for MN066. Is TE-1 revised? A7. Yes. TE-1, line 5 (MN066), Bldgs in TE-2 is revised from 2 to 1.

Q8. How many mowing events should be priced for the semi-improved areas? A8. Three (3) per season (PWS 5.5(d); Section B). Improved grounds remain sixteen (16) mowing, eight (8) trimming and two (2) edging events.

Q9. Is the covered walk between Eagan Buildings 1 and 2 cleaned? A9. The covered walk is swept and its snow is cleared as part of the Eagan Shovel / Blow area (TE-3); it is not part of either building’s Gross SF or Custodial SF.
