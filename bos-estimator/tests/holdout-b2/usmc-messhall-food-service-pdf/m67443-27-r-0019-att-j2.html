PART 1 – GENERAL

1.1 Purpose. These Technical Exhibits (TE) provide the facility data, hours of operation, estimated workload and sanitation requirements for garrison food service at the mess halls of MCB Camp Halloran and MCAS Pell River. The estimated quantities of work listed in the Technical Exhibits are provided for planning and pricing purposes only. The Government does not guarantee any minimum or maximum headcount.

1.2 Scope. This requirement covers sixteen (16) operating garrison mess halls and two (2) satellite facilities listed in TE-1 with a Service Type of FFS, M&MA or SAT. Two service types apply:

  1. Full Food Service (FFS). The Contractor provides all management, supervision, cooks, bakers, mess attendants, cashiers and headcount personnel necessary to requisition, receive, store, prepare, cook and serve all meals, and performs all sanitation of the facility.
  2. Management and Mess Attendant (M&MA). Marine Food Service Specialists (MOS 3381) prepare and cook all meals; M&MA mess halls are designated to provide a training platform for Marine food service personnel. The Contractor provides management support, serving line attendants, dining room, scullery and pot-and-pan attendants, cashiers and headcount personnel, and performs all sanitation of the facility.
  3. Satellite facilities (SAT). Hot meals are prepared at the parent mess hall shown in TE-1 and transported in insulated food containers to the satellite, where the Contractor serves the meal, records headcount and performs all sanitation of the satellite.

In every facility in scope the Contractor performs the sanitation and custodial services of TE-5 throughout the Sanitation Area shown in TE-1, Column (9), on each day of operation.

1.3 Definitions. Headcount – one meal served to one authorized diner, recorded by the Contractor cashier in the Marine Corps Food Management Information System (MCFMIS) by category (meal card, Cash Meal Regular, reservist, other). Meal period – a scheduled serving window shown in TE-2. Midrats – midnight rations served 2300–0100 (2330–0100 at the Flight Line Satellite) to personnel on duty or night shifts; Midrats are a separate meal period. Brunch / Supper – the two meal periods served on Saturdays, Sundays and Federal holidays at mess halls not on a training schedule. Training schedule – breakfast, lunch and dinner served seven days a week to students in a formal course of instruction. Field day – the weekly top-to-bottom cleaning of the facility (TE-5).

PART 2 – BASIS OF ESTIMATES

2.1 Source. TE-3 shows the average headcount per meal period for a typical non-holiday weekday (Monday through Friday) and for a typical Saturday, Sunday or Federal holiday at each facility. The averages are taken from MCFMIS headcount reports (NAVMC 565-1) for the period 1 July 2025 through 30 June 2026, adjusted by the Government for known unit relocations (the reassignment of Bldg TC-410 diners to Bldg TC-575 on 5 January 2026).

2.2 Pricing basis. Offerors shall price fifty-two (52) typical weeks per year at the TE-3 averages. A typical week is five (5) weekdays plus two (2) weekend days at facilities open seven days a week; five (5) weekdays plus one (1) Saturday at Bldg W-133 (Monday through Saturday); and five (5) weekdays at facilities open Monday through Friday. Federal holidays are served on the weekend and holiday schedule and are reflected in the TE-3 averages; Offerors shall not price Federal holidays as additional weekend days.

2.3 Historical data. TE-4 shows total meals served by fiscal year as recorded in MCFMIS. It is provided for information only and reflects deployments, closures and openings described in its remarks. TE-4 is not the pricing basis.

2.4 Workload changes. Unit deployments, training cycles and relocations cause headcount to vary. A change of fifteen percent (15%) or less in the average headcount of a meal period, measured over any ninety (90) consecutive days, is within the scope of the contract price. Changes greater than 15% will be addressed under the Changes clause.

QUESTIONS AND ANSWERS

Q1. Is the area of a satellite facility included in the area of its parent mess hall, and is satellite headcount included in the parent’s headcount?
A1. No to both. The satellites (SP-24 and AS-219) are separate buildings with their own TE-1 area and TE-3 headcount, and are priced as separate line items (TE-1 Note 7; TE-3 Note 5).

Q2. Will Bldg TC-410 reopen during the contract? Should Offerors price it?
A2. Reopening of TC-410 is not scheduled. Do not price TC-410. If it reopens, the Government will add it by modification and the TC-575 workload will be adjusted.

Q3. Is the Gross SF or the Sanitation Area the area to be cleaned?
A3. The Sanitation Area (TE-1, Column 9). Gross SF is the iNFADS building area and includes spaces the Contractor does not clean (TE-1 Note 2). Dining Room SF is part of the Sanitation Area, not in addition to it.

Q4. Does Mess Hall 227 serve Midrats on weekends?
A4. Yes. Mess Hall 227 and the Pell River Mess Hall (AS-4150) serve Midrats seven days a week, as shown in TE-2 and TE-3. The Flight Line Satellite (AS-219) serves Midrats Monday through Friday only.

Q5. Bldg W-133 shows weekend headcount but is closed on Sundays. How should the weekend figures be applied?
A5. W-133 is open Monday through Saturday. Its weekend and holiday columns are Saturday averages and apply to one day per week (paragraph 2.2; TE-3 Note 3). It is closed on Sundays and Federal holidays.

Q6. Please confirm the current contract value and the amount invoiced to date.
A6. See Part 3, paragraph 3.2: Contract No. M67443-22-C-0004 (Longleaf Garrison Dining, LLC), total value $298,746,215 including all option periods, as modified through P00011; Option Period 4 value $63,215,480. Total invoiced from 1 October 2022 through 30 September 2026 (48 months) was $233,418,605. The FAR 52.217-8 estimate ($31,607,740) and the special-event BPA (ceiling $3,500,000) are not part of the contract value.

Q7. Will the Government provide Thanksgiving and Christmas meal headcounts?
A7. Holiday-meal headcounts are reflected in the TE-3 weekend and holiday averages (paragraph 2.2). No separate holiday workload will be provided.

Q8. Does the Contractor maintain the grounds around the mess halls?
A8. No. Grounds maintenance is performed by the Government (TE-5, paragraph 5.2). The Contractor only polices litter within 50 feet of each facility.

Q9. Is the optional line item for Bldg D-1720 evaluated?
A9. Yes. The optional line item is evaluated in accordance with Section M, but it is not part of the base requirement and may never be exercised. The TE-3A projection is the pricing basis for that line item only.
