Item 6 – Questions and Answers

Q1. Is the bid to be priced on gross or cleanable square footage?

A1. Cleanable. Gross square footage is provided for information only (ITB 2.3 and Attachment A Note 1). The Part 2 unit price is per cleanable square foot per month.

Q2. Who is the incumbent contractor and what has the County spent under the current contract?

A2. The current contractor is Gulfview Facility Services, Inc., Contract No. C21-0457, awarded December 14, 2021 under ITB 21-031 (term February 1, 2022 through January 31, 2027, including both renewals). Expenditures for fiscal year 2025-26 (October 1, 2025 – September 30, 2026) were $1,146,820.00. Total expenditures from February 1, 2022 through September 30, 2026 were $4,968,515.40. The current contract covers 28 of the facilities in this ITB, does not include day porter service at the Judicial Center, and includes Additional Services work orders, so these amounts should not be used as a basis for pricing.

Q3. Can the County provide the number of toilets, urinals and sinks in each building?

A3. No. The RESTROOMS column in Attachment A is the number of restroom rooms (Note 3). Fixture counts have not been inventoried; bidders were able to observe restrooms during the site visits.

Q4. Attachment A shows the Judicial Center as 5 floors. Does that include the basement?

A4. Yes: four floors above grade plus the basement. The holding cells, sally port and secure prisoner corridors in the basement are excluded and are not in the 128,640 cleanable square feet.

Q5. Will the Mosquito Control Office be serviced until its staff move out?

A5. No. Facility 309 is deleted (Item 1.5) and will not be serviced under the new contract.

Q6. Are the Option Items evaluated, and will O-2 be added at contract start?

A6. No to both. See ITB 1.6 and 2.10 and Item 4 above.

Q7. Attachment A lists Tue-Sat for the Cypress Point Branch Library. Is that correct?

A7. Yes. The branch is closed Sunday and Monday and is serviced five days per week, Tuesday through Saturday (ITB 2.4).

Q8. Which supplies are furnished by the County?

A8. Toilet tissue, paper hand towels, hand soap and toilet seat covers. The Contractor furnishes can liners, all chemicals, floor finish and equipment (ITB 2.8).

Q9. Is the gym floor at the Westshore Community Center (207) to be sanded and refinished?

A9. No. Only an annual screen and recoat in July (Periodic Services tab). The 7,200 SF wood floor is part of the facility's 18,900 cleanable square feet, not additional area.

Q10. May a bidder exclude a facility from a Group?

A10. No. A bid for a Group must include every facility in that Group as amended by this addendum (ITB 1.6). Bidders may bid one or more Groups.

Q11. Are the park restroom buildings seasonal?

A11. No. Facilities 404 and 405 are serviced 7 days per week year-round; Facility 404 receives a second daily service from Memorial Day through Labor Day, which is included in the monthly price. Facility 406 is serviced Saturday and Sunday year-round.

Q12. Is cleaning of the Emergency Operations Center during a hurricane activation included in the monthly price?

A12. No. Routine Mon-Fri service is included; cleaning during an activation is ordered as Additional Services at the Part 3 hourly rates (ITB 2.9).

Q13. Will the County extend the bid due date?

A13. No. Bids remain due Thursday, November 12, 2026, 2:00 p.m.
