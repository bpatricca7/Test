Section 1 – General Information

1.1 Purpose

The Coquina County Board of County Commissioners (the "County") invites sealed bids from qualified janitorial contractors to furnish all labor, supervision, equipment, cleaning chemicals and materials (except the paper products and hand soap furnished by the County under 2.8) necessary to provide routine and periodic janitorial services at the County facilities listed in Attachment A. The facilities are organized in four Groups (A through D) for bidding and award purposes.

1.2 Background and Current Contract

Coquina County is located on Florida's Gulf coast. The County seat, Marlow Springs, contains the Government Center campus; branch facilities are located in Westshore, Palm Hollow, Cypress Point and Pine Ridge. Janitorial services at most of the facilities in this ITB are currently provided by Gulfview Facility Services, Inc. under Contract No. C21-0457 (ITB 21-031), awarded by the Board on December 14, 2021 for an initial three-year term beginning February 1, 2022 with two one-year renewals, both of which have been exercised. The current contract expires January 31, 2027 and will not be extended. Questions about current contract pricing or expenditures must be submitted in writing under 1.5.

1.3 Contract Term

The initial term of the contract resulting from this ITB shall be three (3) years, from February 1, 2027 through January 31, 2030. The County may renew the contract for two (2) additional one (1) year periods upon mutual written agreement, for a maximum contract term of five (5) years. Prices are firm for the initial term. At each renewal the Contractor may request an adjustment not to exceed the change in the Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers (CPI-U), South Region, for the twelve months ending the preceding October, or three percent (3%), whichever is less.

1.4 Mandatory Pre-Bid Conference and Site Visits

Attendance at the pre-bid conference on October 14, 2026 is mandatory; bids from firms not recorded on the sign-in sheet will be deemed non-responsive. Site visits will follow on October 15 (Groups A and B) and October 16 (Groups C and D), leaving from the County Administration Building at 9:00 a.m. Site visits are strongly recommended but not mandatory. Secure areas of the Judicial Center, Sheriff's Office and 911 Center will be shown only with escort.

1.5 Questions and Addenda

All questions shall be submitted in writing to the Procurement Agent no later than 5:00 p.m. on October 20, 2026, and should cite the ITB section or Attachment A facility number to which they refer. Answers, and any changes to this ITB or to Attachment A, will be issued only by written addendum posted on the County's e-procurement portal. Oral explanations are not binding. Each bidder must acknowledge every addendum on the Bid Form (Attachment B); failure to do so may cause the bid to be deemed non-responsive.

1.6 Basis of Award

Award will be made by Group to the lowest responsive and responsible bidder for each Group, based on the Part 1 monthly price for that Group multiplied by thirty-six (36) months. A bidder may bid one, several or all Groups, but a bid for a Group must include every facility in that Group as listed in Attachment A as amended by addenda. Part 2 unit prices, Part 3 Option Items and Additional Services rates are not evaluated, but must be completed. The County reserves the right to award by individual Group or combination of Groups, to waive minor irregularities, and to reject any or all bids when in the best interest of the County.

1.7 Bid Submission

Bids shall be submitted electronically through the County's e-procurement portal before the due date and time on the cover. Each submission shall include: (a) the completed Bid Form (Attachment B), signed by an authorized officer; (b) the Required Forms in Attachment C, including the Sworn Statement under s. 287.133(3)(a), Florida Statutes, on Public Entity Crimes, the E-Verify Affidavit, the Scrutinized Companies Certification and the Anti-Human Trafficking Affidavit (s. 787.06(13), F.S.); (c) a list of three (3) janitorial contracts of at least 100,000 cleanable square feet each performed within the last five years, with references; (d) a current Florida Division of Corporations registration; and (e) a certificate of insurance or letter from the bidder's insurer per Section 5.

Section 2 – Scope of Services

2.1 General

The Contractor shall clean each facility on the service days and within the service window shown in Attachment A, to the standards in this Section, so that each facility presents a clean, sanitary and orderly appearance at the start of each business day. Services are performed after hours unless Attachment A shows otherwise (libraries, Animal Services, 911 Center and park restroom buildings are cleaned in the morning or mid-day; the Judicial Center also has two day porters).

2.2 Facilities Included and Excluded

The facilities, addresses, number of floors, gross and cleanable square footage, number of restrooms, service days, service windows and remarks are listed in Attachment A – Facility List and Service Schedule (Excel). Section 3 summarizes the Groups. The following County facilities are not part of this ITB: the Coquina County Detention Facility (cleaned by the inmate work program); the fourteen (14) Fire Rescue stations (cleaned by station personnel); the Coquina County Civic Center (operated under a separate management agreement); and the Marlow Springs Airport terminal (Airport Authority). Areas within listed facilities that are excluded are named in the Remarks column of Attachment A.

2.3 Square Footage; Additions and Deletions

Gross square feet is the gross building area from the County's real property inventory and is provided for information only. Cleanable square feet is the area to be serviced by the Contractor; it excludes mechanical and electrical rooms, elevator and utility shafts, unconditioned storage and the excluded areas noted in Attachment A. Cleanable square feet is the basis of the Part 2 unit price. Square footages are believed accurate, but bidders are responsible for verifying conditions at the site visits; no adjustment will be made after award for differences of less than five percent (5%) in any facility.

The County may add or delete facilities, or areas within facilities, at any time during the term of the contract. The monthly price for the affected Group will be increased or decreased by the change in cleanable square feet multiplied by the Contractor's Part 2 unit price per cleanable square foot per month for that Group, effective on the date stated in the County's written change order. Any facility added at a service frequency different from the Group's predominant schedule will be priced by mutual agreement.

2.5 Routine Services

The following tasks are performed at every facility on each service day (column "Each service"), and weekly or monthly as shown. Weekly and monthly tasks at facilities serviced fewer than five days per week are performed on the first service day of the week or month.

FrequencyTasks
Each serviceEmpty all waste and recycling containers and replace liners; clean and disinfect restrooms (toilets, urinals, lavatories, partitions, dispensers, mirrors) and refill County-furnished supplies; spot-clean entrance glass; dust-mop and damp-mop hard floors; vacuum carpeted traffic areas; clean and disinfect drinking fountains, break-room counters and sinks; remove litter within 25 feet of building entrances and empty exterior receptacles at entrances; after any evening meeting in the BOCC Chambers (Facility 101), clean the Chambers before the next business day.
WeeklyVacuum all carpeted areas wall to wall; dust horizontal surfaces to 72 inches; clean interior partition glass; damp-wipe doors, frames and kick plates; scrub restroom floors with an auto-scrubber or machine where accessible.
MonthlyHigh dusting above 72 inches (vents, ledges, light fixtures); clean baseboards; vacuum upholstered furniture; wash waste containers; descale restroom fixtures.

2.6 Periodic Services

Periodic floor care and interior glass cleaning are performed at the frequencies (times per year) on the "Periodic Services" tab of Attachment A and are included in the monthly price. The Contractor shall schedule periodic work with each Facility Manager at least ten (10) days in advance. Strip-and-refinish shall apply a minimum of four (4) coats of finish; scrub-and-recoat a minimum of two (2). Wood gymnasium floor screen-and-recoat at the Westshore Community Center (Facility 207) is performed in July, when the gymnasium is closed; full sanding and refinishing is not part of this contract.

2.7 Clinical Areas and Restroom Disinfection

Examination rooms, dental operatories and clinical restrooms at the Department of Health clinic (Facility 209) shall be cleaned with an EPA-registered hospital-grade disinfectant, following the OSHA Bloodborne Pathogens Standard (29 CFR 1910.1030). The Contractor shall not handle sharps containers or regulated medical waste. The pharmacy and laboratory are excluded.

2.8 Supplies and Equipment

The County furnishes toilet tissue, paper hand towels, hand soap and toilet seat covers, stored at each facility. The Contractor furnishes all can liners, cleaning chemicals (Green Seal GS-37 or equivalent where available), floor finish, equipment, carts and tools, and is responsible for maintaining a two-week stock of County-furnished supplies in each facility's janitor closet by notifying the Facility Manager when stock is low.

2.9 Additional Services

The County may order work outside the routine schedule – including cleaning during Emergency Operations Center activations, Supervisor of Elections ballot warehouse cleaning before and after each election, post-storm cleanup and special events – at the hourly rates in Part 3 of the Bid Form. Additional Services require a written work order from the Facilities Management Division before work begins.

2.10 Option Items

Attachment A lists two optional facilities below the Group totals: O-1, the Coquina County Fairgrounds Exhibition Hall (event cleaning, priced per event), and O-2, the Bayside Branch Library, now under construction. Option Items are priced on Part 3 of the Bid Form, are not part of any Group, are not evaluated for award, and become part of the contract only if the County issues a written change order.

2.11 Hurricanes and Declared Emergencies

During a declared state of emergency the County may suspend services at any facility. The monthly price will be reduced pro rata for each scheduled service not performed. Services resume within twenty-four (24) hours of the County's notice.

Section 4 – Contractor Personnel and Performance

4.1 Project Manager and Supervision

The Contractor shall designate a full-time Project Manager, reachable by telephone during all service windows, and shall have an on-site working supervisor at each Group during evening service. The County does not prescribe staffing levels; bidders are responsible for staffing to meet the specifications.

4.2 Day Porters

Two (2) day porters shall be on site at the Judicial Center (Facility 102), Monday through Friday, 7:30 a.m. to 4:00 p.m., to restock restrooms, respond to spills and keep public areas, jury assembly and courtroom galleries clean. Day porter cost is included in the Group A monthly price.

4.3 Background Screening

All employees assigned to the Judicial Center (102), Sheriff's Office facilities (301, 302) and the 911 Center (303) must pass a Level 2 background screening through the Florida Department of Law Enforcement and the CJIS Security Awareness training before starting work, at the Contractor's expense. All other employees are subject to a County criminal history check. The Contractor shall participate in E-Verify under s. 448.095, F.S., for all employees and subcontractors.

4.4 Inspections and Performance Deductions

The Facility Manager may inspect at any time. Deficiencies reported by 9:00 a.m. shall be corrected by 2:00 p.m. the same day (restrooms within two hours). A deficiency not corrected on time, or a missed service, may result in a deduction of $150.00 per occurrence; a missed day-porter shift, $300.00. Three or more uncorrected deficiencies at a facility in a month constitute grounds for a cure notice.

Section 5 – Insurance and Contract Terms

5.1 Insurance

The Contractor shall maintain, at its own expense, with insurers authorized in Florida and rated A- VII or better by A.M. Best: (a) Commercial General Liability, $1,000,000 per occurrence and $2,000,000 general aggregate, naming Coquina County Board of County Commissioners as additional insured; (b) Business Automobile Liability, $1,000,000 combined single limit; (c) Workers' Compensation as required by Chapter 440, F.S., and Employer's Liability of $500,000; and (d) Employee Dishonesty / Third-Party Crime coverage of $100,000 per occurrence. No performance bond is required.

5.2 Invoicing and Payment

The Contractor shall invoice monthly in arrears, one invoice per Group, listing each facility and any deductions or Additional Services work orders. Payment will be made in accordance with the Local Government Prompt Payment Act, Part VII of Chapter 218, F.S.

5.3 Public Records

The Contractor shall comply with s. 119.0701, F.S. IF THE CONTRACTOR HAS QUESTIONS REGARDING THE APPLICATION OF CHAPTER 119, FLORIDA STATUTES, TO THE CONTRACTOR'S DUTY TO PROVIDE PUBLIC RECORDS RELATING TO THIS CONTRACT, CONTACT THE CUSTODIAN OF PUBLIC RECORDS AT (352) 555-0100, records@coquinacountyfl.example.org, 200 N. PALMETTO AVENUE, MARLOW SPRINGS, FL 34611.

5.4 Statutory Requirements

  1. Public Entity Crimes: a person or affiliate placed on the convicted vendor list may not submit a bid or perform work as provided in s. 287.133(2)(a), F.S.
  2. Scrutinized Companies: the County may terminate the contract if the Contractor is placed on a list described in s. 287.135, F.S.
  3. E-Verify: s. 448.095, F.S., applies to the Contractor and all subcontractors.
  4. Anti-Human Trafficking: the Contractor shall provide the affidavit required by s. 787.06(13), F.S., with the bid and at each renewal.

5.5 Termination

The County may terminate the contract for convenience on sixty (60) days' written notice, or for cause if the Contractor fails to cure a default within ten (10) days of a written cure notice. Funding for each fiscal year (October 1 – September 30) is subject to appropriation by the Board.

5.6 Transition

The Contractor shall be fully staffed and badged at all facilities on February 1, 2027. During the last thirty (30) days of the contract the Contractor shall cooperate with any successor contractor, including walk-throughs and return of keys and access cards.
