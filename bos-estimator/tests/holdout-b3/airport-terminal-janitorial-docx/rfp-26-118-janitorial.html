SECTION 1 – GENERAL INFORMATION

1.1 Purpose

The Ashbury County Regional Airport Authority (“Authority”) requests proposals for janitorial services at the Ashbury County Regional Airport (“Airport”), Ashbury, Wisconsin: continuous (24-hour) coverage of terminal public and sterile areas, nightly and scheduled cleaning of non-public areas and support buildings, restroom servicing, floor care, passenger boarding bridges, the Federal Inspection Services (FIS) facility after international arrivals, and the terminal curbside sidewalks.

1.2 The Airport

The Airport is a small-hub commercial service airport with five passenger airlines and 1,146,318 enplanements in 2025. The Passenger Terminal (Building 1) opened in 1998 and was expanded in 2014 with Concourse B and the FIS facility. It contains approximately 486,000 gross square feet on three levels and 14 gates (A1–A8 and B1–B6), all with passenger boarding bridges. Not all terminal space is included in the scope of services: approximately 335,000 square feet is in the Service Areas listed in Exhibit A, Part 1. The balance is airline, concession, rental car and federal agency space, baggage handling areas and mechanical rooms (Section 2.7 and Exhibit A, Part 3). The terminal Service Areas include roughly 185,000 SF of terrazzo, 83,000 SF of carpet and 18 public restroom groups. The support facilities are listed in Exhibit A, Part 2.

1.3 Contract Term

The initial term is three (3) years, from April 1, 2027 through March 31, 2030, with two (2) one-year renewal options exercisable at the Authority's sole discretion, for a maximum term of five (5) years. Prices for renewal years may be adjusted only as provided in Section 3.3.

1.4 Current Contract

Janitorial services are currently provided by Kinnick & Vail Building Services, Inc. under Contract No. ACRAA-C-22-009, awarded by the Board of Commissioners in February 2022 for a three-year initial term beginning April 1, 2022, with two one-year renewals, both of which have been exercised. The current contract expires March 31, 2027. Buildings 230 and 510 and the curbside sidewalks are currently cleaned under separate purchase orders that will end when the new contract begins.

1.5 Questions and Addenda

The facility tour includes the sterile area and the apron level of both concourses (photo ID and TSA screening required). Answers to written questions and any changes to this RFP will be issued by addendum. Each addendum becomes part of this RFP and shall be acknowledged on Form 2.

SECTION 2 – SCOPE OF SERVICES

2.1 General

The Contractor shall furnish all management, supervision, labor, equipment, chemicals (Green Seal GS-37 or equivalent), liners and materials to keep the Service Areas clean, sanitary and presentable at all times. The Authority furnishes restroom paper products and hand soap. Work is coordinated through the Airport Operations Center (AOC), which dispatches spill calls and work orders. All employees must hold an Authority SIDA badge (fingerprint-based records check and TSA Security Threat Assessment under 49 CFR Part 1542; badge fee $85, lost badge $150).

2.2 Service Areas and Square Footage

The areas to be cleaned are listed in Exhibit A. Part 1 lists the Passenger Terminal (Building 1) by Service Area (zone), not by room, with the floor area of each Service Area broken down by finish; the Total SF column is the area to be serviced. Part 2 lists the support facilities by building with the gross building area (for information) and the Contract Cleaning Area (the area to be serviced). Part 3 lists areas not included, for information only, and Part 4 the exterior areas. Square footages are from the Authority's CAD space inventory (2025 update), are approximate and are provided for reference only. No price adjustment will be made for differences of five percent (5%) or less.

2.4 Terminal Operations and Restrooms

The terminal is open 24 hours. Ticketing opens at 03:30, and the concourses are closed from 01:00 to 04:00 except during irregular operations. Restrooms in 24/7 Service Areas shall be inspected and serviced at least every thirty (30) minutes from 04:00 to 01:00 and at least hourly from 01:00 to 04:00. The Contractor shall respond to spill and cleanup calls from the AOC within ten (10) minutes in the terminal and within thirty (30) minutes at the support facilities.

2.5 Federal Inspection Services (FIS)

The FIS facility (Areas F-01 and F-02) receives seasonal international charter and scheduled flights, currently averaging four (4) arrivals per week from December through April and two (2) per week from May through November. FIS areas shall be cleaned on each arrival day (“Per Arrival”) and are priced per arrival day in Exhibit B, Line 7. CBP administrative offices and hold rooms (Area F-03) are not part of the base scope; see Section 2.8.

2.7 Areas Not Included

The following are cleaned by others and are not included, even where they are inside or next to a Service Area: (a) airline ticket offices, baggage service offices, airline operations offices, the airline club room and the baggage make-up and inbound baggage rooms (the Contractor cleans the public side of the ticket counters in Area T2-01); (b) concession leaseholds within the lease line (the Contractor cleans the common seating in Area T2-03); (c) TSA checkpoint lanes, offices and break rooms (the Contractor cleans the queue and recompose areas, Area T2-04); (d) rental car counters and back offices in the terminal and Building 300, and the Rental Car QTA facility (Building 310); (e) the FAA tower (Building 700) and the General Aviation Terminal (Building 710, FBO leasehold); (f) parking decks, ramps and cashier lanes; (g) ARFF apparatus bays, dormitory and kitchen; and (h) mechanical, electrical, telecommunications and elevator machine rooms. Exhibit A, Part 3 lists the approximate area of these spaces for information only.

2.8 Option Item 1 – CBP Administrative Suite

The Authority may, by written notice at any time during the term, add cleaning of the CBP administrative offices and hold rooms (Area F-03, approximately 3,420 SF, Daily (7) coverage) under the Authority's reimbursable services agreement with CBP. Option Item 1 is priced separately in Exhibit B, Line 11. It is not included in the base Service Areas or in the Exhibit A totals, and it is evaluated as described in Section 3.2.

2.9 Changes in Service Areas

The Authority reserves the right to add or delete Service Areas or buildings, and to change Janitorial Coverage, at any time during the contract. Adjustments shall be priced at the per-square-foot rate derived from the applicable Exhibit B line. The Level 2 concessions redevelopment program may change areas on Level 2 during the term. Building 800 (former air cargo building) is vacant, is scheduled for demolition, and receives no service.

2.10 Exterior Areas

The Contractor shall police the exterior areas in Exhibit A, Part 4 for litter and gum, empty curbside receptacles, and pressure wash the sidewalks monthly from April through October. Snow and ice removal is not included (Authority Airfield Maintenance).
