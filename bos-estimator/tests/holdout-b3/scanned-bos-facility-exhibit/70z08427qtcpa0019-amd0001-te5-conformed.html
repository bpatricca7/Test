SECTION C – DESCRIPTION/SPECIFICATIONS/STATEMENT OF WORK

A. Performance Work Statement (Attachment #1), Technical Exhibit 5, is revised as follows:

(1) Item 11, Building T-47, Temporary Classroom Building (8,640 Gross SF; 7,920 Service Area SF), is DELETED. The building is scheduled for demolition.

(2) Item 7, Building 17, Seamanship Training Building: the Service Schedule Code is changed from “D” to “A”.

(3) Item 14, Building 3, Personnel Support Building: the Service Area SF is changed from 18,450 to 22,610. The second floor training wing is added to the service area.

(4) Building M-115, Modular Classroom, one floor, 2,880 Gross SF, 2,640 Service Area SF, Service Schedule Code “A”, is ADDED to Area I as Item 11A.

(5) The Area I and Area II subtotals and the Training Center total are revised accordingly. The number of buildings to be serviced remains 31. The revised total Service Area SF is 381,210.

A conformed copy of Technical Exhibit 5 incorporating the above changes in ink is attached (pages 3 through 5). Offerors shall price the conformed Technical Exhibit 5.

B. QUESTIONS AND ANSWERS

Q1. Building 41 is listed in Technical Exhibit 5 without a Service Area. Should it be priced?

A1. No. Building 41 is vacant pending renovation and is listed for information only (Note 8). If service is required after the renovation it will be added by modification under PWS paragraph 5.7.

Q2. Do the Service Area figures for the recruit barracks (Buildings 11, 12 and 13) include the berthing compartments and heads?

A2. No. Recruits perform the routine cleaning of berthing compartments and heads. The Service Area shown for these buildings is the passageways, ladderwells and quarterdecks, which receive periodic services only (Code “P”, Note 5).

Q3. Is Building 118 part of the base requirement?

A3. No. Building 118 is under construction. Service will be ordered under optional CLIN 0005 if and when the building is accepted by the Government. Do not include Building 118 in the base or option year prices for CLINs 0001 through 0004 (see Note 9).

Q4. Is this a new requirement? Please identify the incumbent contractor and the value of the current contract.

A4. This is a follow-on requirement. The current contract, 70Z08422CTCPA0007, was awarded to Seawall Facility Services, LLC in September 2022 for a 12-month base period and four 12-month option periods (60 months). The total value of the current contract, including all options, is $5,862,340.

Q5. What is the period of performance of the new contract?

A5. One 12-month base period and four 12-month option periods. See Section F.

Q6. Should offerors price the Gross SF or the Service Area SF?

A6. Price the Service Area SF (column 6). Gross SF (column 5) is taken from the Coast Guard real property inventory and is provided for information only (Notes 1 and 2).
