PERFORMANCE WORK STATEMENT
JANITORIAL AND GROUNDS MAINTENANCE SERVICES

National Park Service · Tamarack Lakes National Recreation Area · Aitkin and Itasca Counties, Minnesota

PART 1 – GENERAL

1.1 Location. Tamarack Lakes National Recreation Area (TALA) is located 14 miles north of Tamarack, Minnesota, on State Highway 65. The park consists of 61,800 acres, 9,400 of which are lake, and received approximately 412,000 recreation visits in 2025. Park Headquarters and the Lakeview Visitor Center are located at mile 0.4 of the Park Road:

National Park Service
Tamarack Lakes National Recreation Area
4120 Lakeview Visitor Center Road
Tamarack, Minnesota

1.2 Background. TALA operates a year-round visitor center and headquarters, a 96-site campground with two shower houses, a swim beach, two day use areas, a marina operated by a concessioner, and vault toilets at trailheads, overlooks and boat accesses. Campgrounds, day use areas and most restroom buildings are open seasonally; when the water systems are winterized, only the year-round buildings and selected vault toilets remain open. All flush toilets are on the park wastewater system; vault toilets are pumped under a separate contract.

1.3 Scope. The Contractor shall provide all management, supervision, labor, transportation, equipment, cleaning chemicals and materials (except as stated in 1.8) necessary to perform (a) janitorial services at the facilities listed in Technical Exhibit 1, Facility Schedule, at the frequencies stated there, and (b) grounds maintenance at the areas listed in Technical Exhibit 3, Grounds Maintenance Schedule. The Contractor shall perform the work in a manner that maintains the sanitary condition and the appearance of park facilities and protects park resources.

1.4 Period of Performance. This requirement is for a base year, with the potential for up to four option years, exercised at the discretion of the National Park Service:

1.6 Hours of Work. Work at the Visitor Center and Headquarters shall be performed between 06:00 and 09:00 or after 16:30, except restroom service required 2X/DAY. Restroom buildings in campgrounds and day use areas shall be serviced between 07:00 and 13:00 so that facilities are clean for afternoon and evening use. Quiet hours in Birch Point Campground (22:00 – 06:00) shall be observed.

1.7 Holidays. Facilities scheduled 7X/WK are serviced on Federal holidays. Facilities scheduled 5X/WK (M–F) are not serviced on Federal holidays; work scheduled on a holiday shall be performed the workday before or the workday after.

1.8 Government-Furnished Supplies. The Government will furnish toilet tissue, paper towels, hand soap, hand sanitizer, trash liners and vault-toilet deodorant, stored at the Maintenance Shop (Bldg. 120). The Contractor shall furnish all other cleaning chemicals, which shall be EPA Safer Choice or biobased products approved by the COR, and all equipment, including mowers, tractors, trimmers, blowers and a boat (with operator) for Pine Island (VT-58).

1.9 Wage Determination. Service Contract Act Wage Determination No. 2015-5013, Revision 27 (Minnesota – Aitkin, Itasca Counties) applies. Janitor (11150) and Laborer, Grounds Maintenance (11030) are the principal classes.

PART 2 – JANITORIAL SERVICES

2.1 Facilities. Technical Exhibit 1 lists 34 buildings and structures. Facilities with a frequency code (Technical Exhibit 2) in Columns (12) through (14) are serviced under this contract. Rows marked N/S (no service), OPT (option) or CONC (concessioner) are listed for reference and are not serviced unless added by modification. All buildings and structures are single-story except the Lakeview Visitor Center (Bldg. 101, two levels with elevator) and the Park Headquarters (Bldg. 102, two stories). Facility types are:

  1. Visitor center, contact station, administrative and entrance buildings (VC, ADM, FEE): offices, lobbies, exhibit areas, restrooms and break rooms; carpet, resilient tile and sealed concrete floors.
  2. Comfort stations (CS): flush-toilet restroom buildings with sinks, and at Sandy Bay, changing rooms.
  3. Shower houses (SH): campground flush-toilet buildings with shower stalls.
  4. Vault toilet buildings (VT-S single riser, VT-D double riser): precast concrete buildings without water. Cleaning includes risers, seats, floors, walls, vents and screens, removal of trash from vaults by grabber, and restocking; it does not include pumping.
  5. Picnic shelters and fish cleaning station (PS, FCS): open-sided roofed structures. Cleaning includes sweeping the slab, washing tables and counters, cleaning grills and sinks, removing spider webs and emptying trash.

2.2 Square Footage. Column (6), Gross SF, is the building gross area recorded in the NPS Facility Management Software System (FMSS). For picnic shelters and the fish cleaning station, Gross SF is the roofed area measured at the drip line; for vault toilet buildings it is the exterior footprint. Unless a note limits service to part of a building, the entire building is serviced. The Contractor shall bill only for facilities and seasons actually serviced.

2.3 Cleaning Frequencies.

2.3.1 Columns (12) – (14) of Technical Exhibit 1 give the minimum cleaning frequency for each facility in each season, using the codes defined in Technical Exhibit 2. A dash (—) means the facility is closed in that season. The frequencies listed are the minimum and are in no way intended to limit the frequency that may be necessary to meet the performance standards in Part 4.

2.3.2 A frequency followed by “(d)” in the Winter column applies only while the facility is open (for example, North Arm Boat Launch VT-41 is open through November 15).

2.3.3 During the Peak season the main-level public restrooms of the Lakeview Visitor Center (Bldg. 101) shall be serviced 2X/DAY; the rest of the building is cleaned 7X/WK.

2.3.4 Periodic tasks (floor scrubbing and refinishing, pressure washing of comfort station and shower house floors, window washing, carpet extraction) are scheduled in Technical Exhibit 2, Table 2-B.

2.4 Opening and Closing Cleans. Each seasonal facility shall receive one opening clean within the five (5) days before its open date and one closing clean within three (3) days after its close date. The NPS performs water-system start-up and winterization; the Contractor shall not operate valves or drain lines.

2.5 Areas and Services Not Included. The following are not part of this contract:

  1. the Sandy Bay Concession Stand and Beach Store (Bldg. 312) and the North Arm Marina Store and Restrooms (Bldg. 412), which are cleaned by the park concessioners under their concession contracts;
  2. the Fire Cache / Cold Storage building (Bldg. 121), and the shop bays, wash bay, welding area and parts storage of the Maintenance Shop (Bldg. 120) – service in Bldg. 120 is limited to the office, break room and locker/restroom area of approximately 1,240 SF (Technical Exhibit 1, Note (b));
  3. the Pelican Point Comfort Station (Bldg. 413), which is closed and scheduled for demolition;
  4. the Birch Point Group Camp Shower House (Bldg. 214), unless Optional CLIN 0004 is exercised (Note (c));
  5. composting toilets at the 14 backcountry campsites and the 3 portable toilets at the North Arm ice-fishing access, which are serviced by NPS staff or under separate contract;
  6. pumping of vault toilets, septic tanks and lift stations (separate blanket purchase agreement).

2.6 Changes in Facilities. Facilities may be added, deleted or closed during the contract because of construction, high water, fire danger, lapse in appropriations or other conditions. The Contracting Officer (CO) will advise the Contractor in writing as far in advance as possible, but no less than forty-eight (48) hours in advance of stop-service and when service should be resumed. Additions and deletions will be priced by modification using the unit prices for the facility type and season in the Schedule of Items.

2.7 Restocking and Reporting. Dispensers shall be fully stocked at the end of each service. The Contractor shall report plugged fixtures, vandalism, graffiti, wildlife in buildings and vaults more than three-quarters full to the COR by e-mail the same day and shall post an “Out of Order” sign on any unusable fixture.

PART 3 – GROUNDS MAINTENANCE

3.1 Mowing Season. Mowing and trimming are performed from May 1 through October 15 (approximately 24 weeks). Areas, acreages and frequencies are listed in Technical Exhibit 3, Table 3-A. The grounds types are:

  1. Type I – Improved: mow at 3 inches when grass reaches 4 inches, not less than once per week (1X/WK); trim around buildings, signs, tables, fire rings and posts at each mowing; edge walks 1X/MO.
  2. Type II – Semi-improved: mow at 4 – 6 inches once every three weeks (1X/3 WKS, eight cycles per season) with tractor-mounted equipment; no trimming except around signs and guardrail ends.
  3. Type III – Unimproved: mow once per year (1X/YR), not before August 1, to protect nesting grassland birds.

3.2 Paved Areas. Parking lots, the Visitor Center plaza and the Lakeshore Paved Trail (Table 3-B) shall be kept free of litter and debris; parking lots shall be swept with a mechanical sweeper at the frequencies shown. Snow and ice removal is performed by the NPS and is not part of this contract.

3.3 Campsite and Site Policing. The Contractor shall police campsites, remove ashes from fire rings and empty bear-resistant trash and recycling containers at the quantities and frequencies in Table 3-C.

3.4 Grounds Work Not Included. Natural-surface trails (approximately 41 miles), trail brushing, hazard tree removal, beach sand grooming at Sandy Bay, prairie restoration units and the Park Road right-of-way beyond the 8-foot shoulder swath are maintained by NPS crews and are not part of this contract.

3.5 Protection of Resources. Mowing equipment shall not be operated within 50 feet of the lakeshore (except established lawns at Sandy Bay and North Arm) or in the archeological sites flagged by the COR. Clippings shall not be blown into the lake.

PART II – QUESTIONS AND ANSWERS

Q1. Is this a new requirement? Who is the incumbent, and what is the current contract value?
A1. This is a follow-on requirement. Janitorial services are currently provided by North Country Facility Services, LLC under contract 140P6122C0009, awarded in March 2022 for a base year (April 1, 2022 – March 31, 2023) and four option years ending March 31, 2027. The total value of the contract, including all option years exercised, is $2,731,460 over 60 months; the Option Year 4 price (April 1, 2026 – March 31, 2027) is $574,920. The current contract covers janitorial services only. Grounds maintenance is currently performed by NPS staff and is added to this requirement, and the Sandy Bay Beach House (Bldg. 310) was cleaned by NPS seasonal staff until 2026. This information is provided for reference only.

Q2. Technical Exhibit 1 lists Bldg. 120 Maintenance Shop at 8,960 SF. Should the full 8,960 SF be priced?
A2. No. 8,960 SF is the FMSS gross area of the building. Only the office, break room and locker/restroom area, approximately 1,240 SF, is serviced (Note (b) and PWS 2.5(b)).

Q3. Should the Group Camp Shower House (Bldg. 214) be included in the base-year price?
A3. No. Price Bldg. 214 only under Optional CLIN 0004. The Government does not expect to exercise the option before Option Year 2, when the water system replacement is scheduled to be complete.

Q4. Does the contract include pumping of the vault toilets?
A4. No. Vault pumping is performed under a separate blanket purchase agreement (PWS 2.5(f)). The Contractor reports vaults more than three-quarters full (PWS 2.7).

Q5. The Sandy Bay Beach House is scheduled 2X/DAY. Is that seven days a week?
A5. Yes. 2X/DAY is twice daily, 7 days per week, including holidays, for the whole Peak season (Technical Exhibit 2, Table 2-A). The building is closed outside Peak.

Q6. How many mowing cycles should be priced for Type I and Type II areas?
A6. Price 24 cycles for Type I (1X/WK, May 1 – October 15) and 8 cycles for Type II (1X/3 WKS). Type III (G-7) is one cycle per year.

Q7. Are the picnic shelters at Sandy Bay heated or enclosed in winter?
A7. No. All picnic shelters are open-sided and close October 15 or earlier (Technical Exhibit 1, Column 11).

Q8. Will the Government provide a site visit?
A8. A site visit was held on December 1, 2026; 7 firms attended. No further site visits will be held. Facility location maps are in Attachment J-2.
