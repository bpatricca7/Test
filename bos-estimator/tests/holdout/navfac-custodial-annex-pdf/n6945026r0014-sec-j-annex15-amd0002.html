GENERAL NOTES TO J-1503010-09

  1. Facility data (Fac. No., CCN, CCN Title, Flrs and Facility Total SF) are extracted from iNFADS as of 30 June 2026 and are provided for information. Facility Total SF is the gross area of the facility.
  2. Total Custodial SF is the area that receives recurring custodial services and is the sum of the five floor-type columns. Mechanical, electrical and communications rooms, elevator machine rooms, unoccupied storage and the areas excluded in Remarks are not included and will not be serviced. Building Perimeter Services (Spec Item 3.1.5) apply to every facility with a Total Custodial SF.
  3. COL / Class: COL 2 and COL 3 = Common Output Level, J-1503010-03 (spaces designated Prestige are identified in Remarks and receive COL 2); A, B, C, D = Service Class, J-1503010-04 (tenant commands not funded by CNIC); MTF = J-1503010-05; CYP = J-1503010-06.
  4. Service Days are the days on which work items with a frequency of S or D are performed and on which all other work items are scheduled. M–F Monday through Friday; M–Sa Monday through Saturday; M–Su seven days per week; M/W/F Monday, Wednesday and Friday; T/Th Tuesday and Thursday; Wed one day per week (Wednesday). Facilities with M–F, M/W/F, T/Th or Wed service are not serviced on Federal holidays; M–Sa and M–Su facilities are serviced on holidays. A work item whose frequency exceeds the Service Days (e.g., 3W in a Wed facility) is performed on each Service Day.

Subtotals and the grand total include only facilities that receive Recurring Work. Rows annotated with Notes 9 through 12 are listed for information and are excluded. Notes 5 through 13 are on page 3 of this attachment. Rows revised by Amendment 0002 are marked “A0002” in Remarks.
