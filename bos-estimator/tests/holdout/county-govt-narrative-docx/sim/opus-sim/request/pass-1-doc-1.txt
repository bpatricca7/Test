TO WHOM IT MAY CONCERN:
Kessling County is requesting proposals from qualified firms to furnish janitorial services at the County buildings and park facilities described in Section 3 of this Request for Proposals (RFP). Service is to be proposed at all of the locations listed, with a separate price for each location on Attachment B. Proposers are required to attend the pre-proposal meeting and site tour; building square footage, service hours and access will be reviewed during the tour. Sealed proposals marked “RFP No. 2026-041 – Janitorial Services” must be delivered to the Purchasing Division, Room 210, Administration Building, by the due date and time above. SECTION 1 – GENERAL INFORMATION
1.1 Purpose. It is Kessling County’s intent to contract professional cleaning services on an annual basis for the County facilities described in Section 3, Description of Facilities / Locations. The Contractor shall furnish all labor, supervision, equipment, cleaning chemicals and materials necessary to perform the services described in this RFP, except the supplies the County furnishes under Section 2.1.
1.2 Background. Kessling County (2020 population 63,912) is governed by a seven-member Board of Commissioners. The Department of Facilities Management maintains seventeen (17) County buildings totaling approximately 480,000 square feet, and the Parks and Recreation Department operates four (4) park buildings that are included in this RFP. Building maintenance, grounds maintenance and snow removal are performed by County staff and are not part of this RFP.
1.3 Contract Term. The initial term of the contract shall be three (3) years, from February 1, 2027 through January 31, 2030, with the option of two (2) additional one-year extensions upon mutual written agreement. Prices shall remain firm for the first contract year. Price adjustments for the second and later years shall not exceed the change in the Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers (CPI-U), Midwest Region, for the preceding twelve months, and must be requested in writing at least sixty (60) days before the contract anniversary date.
1.4 Pre-Proposal Meeting and Site Tour. A mandatory pre-proposal meeting will be held at the date and time on the cover page. The tour will visit the Downtown Campus and the Justice Complex; Proposers may schedule visits to the outlying and park sites through the Purchasing Agent until November 13, 2026. Proposals will not be accepted from firms that did not attend.
1.5 Questions and Addenda. Questions must be submitted in writing by e-mail to the Purchasing Agent by the deadline on the cover page. Answers, and any changes to this RFP, will be issued only by written addendum posted on the County’s bid page. Proposers shall acknowledge each addendum on Attachment A. Oral statements by County staff are not binding.
1.6 Award. The County intends to award one contract for all sites but reserves the right to award by site group (Downtown Campus, Justice Complex, Outlying Facilities, Parks) or to more than one Contractor, to reject any or all proposals, and to waive informalities.
1.7 Additions and Deletions of Buildings. The County may add or delete buildings, or portions of buildings, at any time during the contract term. Additions and deletions will be priced at the per-square-foot monthly rate stated on Attachment B, Line C-4, unless the County and the Contractor agree on a separate price in writing. The County will give the Contractor at least fifteen (15) days’ notice.
SECTION 2 – SCOPE OF SERVICES
2.1 Supplies. The Contractor shall furnish all cleaning chemicals, floor finish, equipment, mops, vacuum bags and other consumables. The County will furnish toilet tissue, paper towels, hand soap, seat covers and can liners in the specified sizes. All chemicals shall be Green Seal certified or equivalent, and Safety Data Sheets shall be kept in each janitor closet.
2.2 Hours of Service. Unless otherwise specified in Section 3, services shall be performed five (5) evenings per week, Monday through Friday, between the hours of 5:30 p.m. and 11:30 p.m. Work shall not begin in occupied offices before the occupants leave. Days and hours that differ for a particular location are stated in that location’s description in Section 3.
2.3 Holidays. When County buildings are closed for the holidays listed below, no janitorial services are required, except that services are still required at those locations noted as open (Sites 7, 18, 19 and 20). County holidays are: New Year’s Day; Martin Luther King, Jr. Day; Presidents’ Day; Memorial Day; Independence Day; Labor Day; Veterans Day; Thanksgiving Day and the day after; Christmas Eve; Christmas Day; and New Year’s Eve. No reduction in the monthly price will be made for holidays.
2.4 Security and Background Checks. All Contractor employees shall pass a criminal history check through the Michigan State Police ICHAT system before working in any County building. Employees assigned to the Justice Center, Law Enforcement Center and Jail (Sites 6, 7 and 8) shall also be cleared by the Sheriff’s Office through the Law Enforcement Information Network (LEIN), shall complete CJIS security awareness training, and shall wear County-issued identification badges at all times. Keys and access cards are issued by Facilities Management and remain County property; lost keys will be re-cored at the Contractor’s expense.
2.5 Minimum Cleaning Tasks. The following minimum tasks apply at every location. “Each service visit” means each day the location is scheduled for service in Section 3. Locations serviced fewer than five days per week receive the weekly tasks on the first scheduled day of each week.

Task | Frequency
Empty waste and recycling containers; replace liners as needed | Each service visit
Clean and disinfect restrooms, fixtures, partitions and floors; restock paper and soap | Each service visit
Vacuum carpeted traffic lanes and entry mats; spot vacuum offices | Each service visit
Dust mop and damp mop hard-surface floors | Each service visit
Clean entrance glass, drinking fountains and break room counters and sinks | Each service visit
Courtrooms: dust benches, counsel tables, jury box rails and gallery seating | Each service visit
Vacuum all carpet edge to edge, including under desks where accessible | Weekly
High and low dusting (vents, ledges, blinds, baseboards) | Monthly
Spray buff or burnish VCT corridors and lobbies | Monthly
Clean interior partition glass and door side lights | Quarterly
Hot water extraction of carpeted traffic areas | Semi-annually
Strip and refinish VCT (scheduled with Facilities Management) | Annually

2.6 Winter Conditions. From November 1 through April 15, the Contractor shall vacuum entry mats and remove salt and sand residue from entrance floors at each service visit.
2.7 Work Not Included. Exterior window washing, light fixture relamping, carpet and floor repair, pest control, grounds maintenance, lawn care and snow removal are not included in this RFP. Exterior work is limited to sweeping entrance landings and emptying exterior waste containers within fifteen (15) feet of building entrances.
2.8 Inspections and Deficiencies. Facilities Management will inspect each location at least monthly. Deficiencies reported by the County shall be corrected within twenty-four (24) hours. Uncorrected deficiencies will be deducted from the monthly invoice as provided in Section 4.5.

3.1 Downtown Campus (Sites 1–5)
Site 1 – Kessling County Courthouse
301 E. Main Street, Ardmore Falls, MI 49689
Scope of Work: Kessling County Courthouse (approximately 38,900 sq. ft.). The historic Courthouse was built in 1898 and renovated in 1987. It is three stories over a full basement and houses the County Clerk, Register of Deeds, County Treasurer and the historic courtroom on the third floor, which is used for ceremonies, naturalizations and weddings. All common areas, offices and restrooms will be cleaned five (5) nights per week, Monday thru Friday, after 5:00 p.m. The building has one passenger elevator and ten (10) restrooms, four of them public. The marble lobby floor and the oak stair treads shall be dust mopped at each service visit and damp mopped weekly; no stripping chemicals may be used on the marble. The Historical Society archive room in the basement is cleaned by Society volunteers and is excluded. The Courthouse square, approximately 2.3 acres of lawn and walks, is maintained by County grounds staff.
Site 2 – Kessling County Administration Building
Kessling County Administration Building — estimated square footage: 52,400
400 E. Main Street — Evenings: Monday thru Friday
Ardmore Falls, MI 49689
Four-story office building (Lower Level and Floors 1–3), built in 1979 with an addition in 2008. Area by floor: Lower Level 9,800 sq. ft.; First Floor 14,200 sq. ft.; Second Floor 14,200 sq. ft.; Third Floor 14,200 sq. ft. Occupants include County Administration, Human Resources, Equalization, Information Technology, Purchasing and the Board of Commissioners Chambers. Two elevators; fourteen (14) restrooms. Floor finishes are approximately 34,000 sq. ft. of carpet tile, 11,600 sq. ft. of VCT and 6,800 sq. ft. of ceramic tile. The Board of Commissioners meets in the First Floor Chambers on the first and third Tuesday of each month at 6:00 p.m.; the Chambers shall be cleaned after the meeting adjourns. The Lower Level server room is excluded.
Site 3 – Courthouse Annex
315 E. Main Street, Ardmore Falls, MI 49689
Two-story former bank building, 9,860 sq. ft. +/-, occupied by the Veterans Affairs office, MSU Extension and the Drain Commissioner. Service: three (3) evenings per week – Monday, Wednesday and Friday. Four (4) restrooms. The unfinished basement storage area is not counted in the square footage and is not cleaned.
Site 4 – Human Services Building
1220 S. Lincoln Avenue, Ardmore Falls, MI 49689
Two-story building of approximately 41,750 square feet (First Floor 21,420 sq. ft.; Second Floor 20,330 sq. ft.), shared by the Kessling County Health Department (first floor: immunization clinic, WIC, environmental health) and the Michigan Department of Health and Human Services local office (second floor, leased to the State). Service is evenings, Monday thru Friday, after 6:00 p.m. The twelve (12) clinic exam rooms require disinfection of exam tables, counters and sinks at each service visit using an EPA-registered disinfectant approved by the Health Department. The Health Department laboratory and pharmacy are cleaned by Health Department staff and are excluded. Sixteen (16) restrooms, including two (2) family restrooms.
Site 5 – Kessling County Library, Main Library
210 N. Spruce Street, Ardmore Falls, MI 49689
Two-story library of approximately 27,300 sq. ft. with a children’s area, public computer lab, two (2) community meeting rooms and six (6) public restrooms. Library hours are Monday–Thursday 9:00 a.m.–8:00 p.m., Friday and Saturday 9:00 a.m.–5:00 p.m., and Sunday 1:00–5:00 p.m. from September through May. Cleaning is required six (6) days per week, Monday thru Saturday, after closing. No Sunday cleaning is required. Book stacks are dusted by library staff.
3.2 Justice Complex, W. Hudson Road (Sites 6–9)
The Justice Complex is a secured campus. Contractor employees must sign in at the Law Enforcement Center front desk at the start of each shift (see Section 2.4).
Site 6 – Kessling County Justice Center
1700 W. Hudson Road, Ardmore Falls, MI 49689
The Justice Center houses the Circuit Court, District Court, Probate Court, the Prosecuting Attorney and Court Administration. It is a three-story building constructed in 2004 of approximately 71,250 square feet: First Floor 26,150 sq. ft.; Second Floor 24,300 sq. ft.; Third Floor 20,800 sq. ft. The court holding cells, prisoner corridor and secure vehicle sally port on the first floor (approximately 2,100 sq. ft.) are maintained by the Sheriff’s Office and are excluded; the Contractor’s cleaning area is approximately 69,150 sq. ft. The building has six (6) courtrooms, a jury assembly room seating 150, six (6) jury deliberation rooms, eighteen (18) public restrooms and ten (10) staff restrooms. Days and hours of service: evenings, Monday thru Friday, 5:30 p.m. to 11:30 p.m. Courtrooms in session after 5:00 p.m. shall be cleaned after the court adjourns.
Site 7 – Law Enforcement Center (Sheriff’s Office and Central Dispatch)
1720 W. Hudson Road, Ardmore Falls, MI 49689
Two-story building of approximately 24,600 sq. ft. housing Sheriff’s Administration, Road Patrol, the Detective Bureau and Kessling Central Dispatch (911). Service is required seven (7) days per week, including holidays. Central Dispatch operates 24 hours a day; the dispatch floor shall be cleaned between 10:00 p.m. and 2:00 a.m. in coordination with the shift supervisor, and only with a dispatcher present. The evidence and property room is cleaned by Sheriff’s staff only and is excluded.
Site 8 – Kessling County Jail
1740 W. Hudson Road, Ardmore Falls, MI 49689 (connected to the Law Enforcement Center)
The Jail is a two-story, 212-bed facility of approximately 96,800 sq. ft. The secure areas – housing units, booking and intake, the vehicle sally port, kitchen, laundry, medical unit and inmate program rooms – are cleaned by inmate workers under the supervision of Corrections staff and are NOT part of this contract. Contractor services are limited to the public lobby, the visitation lobby and visitor restrooms, and the first-floor jail administration offices, a total of approximately 5,850 sq. ft. Service: evenings, Monday thru Friday. Contractor staff will not enter the secure perimeter.
Site 9 – Friend of the Court and Probation Building
1760 W. Hudson Road, Ardmore Falls, MI 49689
One-story office building, approximately 12,480 sq. ft., occupied by the Friend of the Court and the Michigan Department of Corrections Adult Probation office. Eight (8) interview rooms, a public lobby and six (6) restrooms, including the drug-testing restroom, which shall be cleaned and disinfected at each service visit. Evenings, Monday thru Friday.

4.6 Termination. Either party may terminate the contract without cause on sixty (60) days’ written notice. The County may terminate for cause on ten (10) days’ written notice if deficiencies are not corrected.
