Notes

1. Purpose. This attachment identifies the buildings and areas in which the Contractor shall provide housekeeping (environmental) services under the Performance Work Statement (PWS), and the estimated cleanable square feet (CSF) of each, for the base period and each option period. The Contractor shall furnish all management, supervision, labor, equipment, supplies and materials, except as stated in the PWS, to maintain the listed areas in a clean, safe and sanitary condition that meets the current standards of The Joint Commission and the Medical Center’s Infection Prevention and Control policies.

2. Gross vs. cleanable square feet. Column (8) Gross SF (GSF) is taken from the VA Capital Asset Inventory and is provided for information only. Columns (9)–(12) are the estimated cleanable square feet (CSF): the floor area the Contractor will clean, measured from the Medical Center’s space data. CSF excludes mechanical, electrical and telecommunications rooms, shafts, penthouses, wall thickness, areas cleaned by other services (Note 7) and any area not listed. Offerors shall base their staffing and pricing on Column (12), Total CSF.

3. Accuracy of data. Square footages are estimates believed accurate to within plus or minus five percent (5%). Offerors are encouraged to attend the site visit and verify areas. Variations within this tolerance will not be the basis for a price adjustment. Areas may be added or deleted during performance by contract modification; unit prices per CSF by area classification will be used to price such changes.

4. Area classifications. Each cleanable area is assigned one of three classifications based on infection risk:

ClassificationTypical areasCleaning standard
High Risk (Critical Care)Operating and procedure rooms, PACU, cardiac cath lab, endoscopy, ICU and step-down, ED treatment and trauma rooms, dialysis treatment floor, SCI wound care, oral surgeryAseptic cleaning; between-case and terminal cleaning on call; daily terminal clean of ORs
Moderate Risk (Patient Care)Inpatient and resident rooms, exam and treatment rooms, nurses’ stations, patient restrooms and showers, waiting rooms, laboratories, imaging, pharmacy, therapy areasDaily disinfection of high-touch surfaces; discharge / terminal cleaning of patient rooms, including isolation rooms
Low Risk (Non-Patient Care)Offices, conference rooms, classrooms, lobbies, corridors outside patient care units, stairwells, elevators, staff restrooms, locker and break roomsRoutine housekeeping

6. Schedules within a building. In Code A and Code B buildings, low-risk office suites are serviced Monday through Friday (Code D) unless noted otherwise; Table B-3 shows the areas of Building 1 that are serviced on Code D. Recognized Federal holidays are the eleven (11) holidays listed in PWS paragraph 1.6. When a holiday falls on a Saturday or Sunday, the preceding Friday or following Monday is observed.

7. Areas and services not included. The following are not part of this contract and are not included in any CSF figure:

  1. Sterile Processing Service (SPS) decontamination, preparation and packaging, and sterile storage areas in Building 1 (11,420 SF), which are cleaned by SPS staff;
  2. Nutrition and Food Service (NFS) food production, tray line and dish room areas in Building 4;
  3. Building 5, Research and Development, including the Veterinary Medical Unit, which is cleaned under a separate Research Service contract;
  4. Veterans Canteen Service (VCS) retail store, Patriot Cafe and VCS storage in Building 15;
  5. out-leased and non-VA operated space: Building 18 (Child Development Center, enhanced-use lease) and Building 19 (Fisher House, operated by the Fisher House Foundation);
  6. Building 20, Parking Garage (412,000 GSF, 1,150 spaces);
  7. Building 13, Boiler Plant, and all mechanical penthouses, which are cleaned by Engineering Service;
  8. Marston CBOC, where janitorial services are provided by the Lessor under the lease;
  9. vacant buildings: Building 21 (Former Nurses’ Quarters) and Building 23 (Former Laundry); and
  10. grounds maintenance, snow and ice removal, parking lot sweeping and exterior trash enclosures, which are performed by Engineering Service. The Contractor is responsible only for building entrances, vestibules and the exterior area within ten (10) feet of each entrance.

8. Domiciliary resident rooms. Residents of the Domiciliary (Buildings 7 and 7A) clean their own rooms as part of the treatment program. Resident rooms (21,640 SF) are included in the GSF of Building 7 but not in its CSF. The Contractor cleans common areas, group rooms, restrooms and showers, and the dining room.

9. Locked and secured units. Contractor employees shall be escorted on the locked units of Building 2 and in the VA Police holding room (Building 14), and shall follow the Medical Center’s environment-of-care rounds for ligature-resistant settings.

10. Temporary buildings. Buildings T-37 and T-38 are temporary modular buildings. They remain in the contract until the Contracting Officer issues a modification deleting them.

11. Optional Line Item 1001 – Building 300. Building 300, New Community Living Center (96,400 GSF; estimated 74,800 CSF; Code A), is under construction with an estimated occupancy in FY2029. It is shown in Group D for planning purposes and is priced only as Optional Line Item 1001. It is not included in any total in this attachment. The Government is not obligated to exercise the optional line item.

12. Column definitions. Column (1) Line No. is a reference number only. Column (5) counts floors above grade; Column (6) shows whether the building has a basement (Y/N). Column (7) shows operating beds (inpatient buildings) or dialysis stations (sta.) and is provided for information only. An asterisk (*) on a Total CSF figure refers to Table B-3.

PART II – QUESTIONS AND ANSWERS

1. Question: Should pricing be based on the gross square footage or the cleanable square footage?

Response: Cleanable square footage. Offerors shall price Column (12), Total CSF, as revised by this Amendment. Column (8) GSF is information only (Attachment B, Note 2).

2. Question: Are the resident rooms in the Domiciliary (Building 7) included in the CSF?

Response: No. Residents clean their own rooms (Note 8). The 21,640 SF of resident rooms is not in the CSF. When a resident is discharged, the Contractor shall perform a move-out clean of the room on request; this is included in the Building 7 price.

3. Question: Please identify the incumbent contractor, the current contract number and the current contract value.

Response: The current contract is 36C25222C0087, awarded on 26 September 2022 to Brightwater Environmental Services Group, LLC for a base year and four option years. Option Year 4 (1 October 2026 – 30 September 2027) was exercised at $7,316,480.00 for the 12-month period. Payments under the contract from 1 October 2022 through 28 February 2027 (53 months) total $30,912,764.18. The scope of the current contract differs from this solicitation; Buildings 102 and 103 were added by modification in 2024.

4. Question: Will the Contractor be required to clean the elevator lobbies or stairwells in the parking garage (Building 20)?

Response: No. Building 20 is excluded in its entirety (Note 7(f)).

5. Question: Is the new Community Living Center (Building 300) part of the base requirement?

Response: No. Building 300 is priced only as Optional Line Item 1001 (Note 11). If the optional line item is exercised, the estimated CSF is 74,800 at Service Schedule Code A, with an estimated occupancy of 1 June 2029.

6. Question: Does the Code A schedule for Building 1 apply to the administrative offices on the 7th floor?

Response: No. Per Note 6 and Table B-3, the 7th floor offices are serviced on Code D. Restrooms and elevator lobbies on the 7th floor follow Code A.

7. Question: What are the hours of the CBOCs, and is Marston CBOC included?

Response: Pine Hollow and Ellery CBOCs are open Monday through Friday, 0730–1630. Tamarack Lake CBOC is open Monday, Wednesday and Friday, 0800–1630. Cleaning may be performed after clinic hours. Marston CBOC is not included; janitorial services there are provided by the Lessor (Note 7(h)).

8. Question: Can CAD floor plans be provided?

Response: CAD files will not be released. Floor plans were available for review at the site visit held 30 March 2027 and will be provided to the awardee after contract award.

9. Question: Who provides paper products, soap and can liners?

Response: The Contractor provides all cleaning chemicals, equipment and can liners. The Government provides paper towels, toilet tissue, hand soap and hand sanitizer refills (PWS paragraph 5.2).

Except as provided herein, all terms and conditions of the document referenced in Item 9A, as heretofore changed, remain unchanged and in full force and effect.
